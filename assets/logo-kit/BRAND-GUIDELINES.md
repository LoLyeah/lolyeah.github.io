# LoLyeah — Brand & Logo Guidelines

> Identity standard for LoLyeah Interactive Guides & Visual Compendiums ([`lolyeah.github.io`](https://lolyeah.github.io)).

---

## 1. The Logo & Visual Concept
- **Concept (Precision Interlock):** A Swiss modernist geometric monogram of **L** and **Y** on an exact 60°/90° integer grid, separated by a calibrated 14px optical negative-space channel.
- **Brand Purpose:** Signals analytical depth, empirical rigor, and interactive systems simulation across clinical medicine, macroeconomics, clean energy, and physical sciences.
- **Key Characteristics:** Zero artificial skew, zero gradient distortion, and zero glow halos. Pure mathematical clarity and contrast.

## 3. Clear Space & Proportions
- **Clear Zone:** Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the width of the `L` stem ($W = 38\text{px}$ on a 256 canvas, or $0.15\times$ total mark width) around the logo on all sides.
- **No Intrusion:** No text, buttons, dividers, or background noise may enter this clear perimeter.

## 7. Absolute Don'ts (Brand Rules)
1. **No Drop Shadows or Glow Halos:** Adhere strictly to DESIGN.md *"Ghost Glow Ban"*. Zero-offset blurred halos are prohibited.
2. **No Rainbow or Gradient Fills:** Do not use linear or radial gradients inside the letterforms; flat color planes ensure flawless reproduction across media.
3. **No Skewing:** Do not angle or italicize the mark. The stems are vertical and mathematically disciplined.
4. **No Re-spacing:** Do not alter the 14px optical channel between L and Y.
5. **No Stretching or Non-Uniform Scaling:** Always preserve the 1:1 symbol aspect ratio.
