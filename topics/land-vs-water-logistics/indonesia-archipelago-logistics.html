Terbelah oleh palung samudra sedalam 7.400 meter, transportasi laut bukanlah moda alternatif—melainkan satu-satunya moda penghubung logistik massal yang memungkinkan secara fisik.

The Great Cost Paradox: Jakarta → Jayapura vs. Guangzhou Paradoks Biaya: Jakarta → Jayapura vs. Guangzhou

Shipping a 20ft container from Jakarta to Jayapura (~3,800 km) cost $2,500–$3,000 USD, while shipping that identical container to Guangzhou (~3,500 km) cost only $700 USD. Why did domestic water logistics fail to deliver Archimedes’ efficiency? Mengirim kontainer 20 kaki dari Jakarta ke Jayapura (~3.800 km) menelan $2.500–$3.000 USD, sementara kontainer yang sama ke Guangzhou (~3.500 km) hanya butuh $700 USD. Mengapa logistik air domestik sempat gagal menghasilkan efisiensi Archimedes?

1. Vessel Scale Disparity International ULCVs carry 20,000 TEUs. Shallow Eastern Indonesian drafts (6.5–8.5m) cap feeders at 800 TEUs, incurring 5× higher fuel and crew overhead per container box. 2. The Empty Backhaul Trap Ships sail eastward 98% full of rice, cement, and consumer goods. Eastern exports are raw bulk commodities shipped on barges. Kontainer balik hanya terisi 18%, memaksa barang pergi menanggung biaya bolak-balik.
FOLIO III • STRUCTURAL STATE INTERVENTION LEMBAR III • INTERVENSI STRUKTURAL NEGARA

The Tol Laut Intervention: Scheduled Liners & Air Bridges Intervensi Tol Laut: Pelayaran Terjadwal & Jembatan Udara

Launched in 2015 as the centerpiece of Indonesia’s Global Maritime Fulcrum doctrine, the Tol Laut program deployed three systemic pillars to dismantle eastern logistics price disparities. Diluncurkan pada 2015 sebagai pilar Poros Maritim Dunia, program Tol Laut menerapkan tiga instrumen sistemik untuk memangkas disparitas harga logistik di wilayah timur.

PILLAR 1

Scheduled Liner Subsidies Subsidi Pelayaran Liner

39+ active subsidized liner routes operated by PT PELNI and ASDP. Guarantees regular weekly departures regardless of outbound load factor, replacing erratic tramp voyages with fixed commercial predictability. 39+ trayek liner bersubsidi yang dioperasikan PT PELNI dan ASDP. Menjamin jadwal keberangkatan mingguan yang pasti terlepas dari tingkat keterisian, menggantikan pelayaran tramper tak menentu.

Result: Predictable supply chains across 3T regencies
PILLAR 2

SITOLPRENT Digital Quota Sistem Digital SITOLPRENT

Digital booking allocation system preventing middleman freight syndicates from monopolizing subsidized container slots. Allocates space directly to verified regional cooperatives and staple merchants. Sistem alokasi kuota digital untuk mencegah kartel calo ekspedisi memonopoli slot kontainer subsidi. Mengunci alokasi langsung ke koperasi daerah dan pedagang sembako terverifikasi.

Result: Eliminated speculative freight rent-seeking
PILLAR 3

Jembatan Udara (Air Bridge) Integrasi Jembatan Udara

Tol Laut vessels offload cargo at coastal gateways (Timika, Merauke). Subsidized pioneer aircraft (Cessna Caravan) airlift cement and food to 60+ isolated Papuan highland valley airstrips without road access. Kapal Tol Laut membongkar muatan di pelabuhan pesisir (Timika, Merauke). Pesawat perintis bersubsidi menerbangkan semen dan sembako ke 60+ lapangan terbang lembah pedalaman Papua.

Result: Highland cement prices cut by 40%–60%
FOLIO IV • OVERLAND COMPETITION LEMBAR IV • PERSAINGAN ANGKUTAN DARAT

Trans-Java Highway, Sunda Ro-Ro & The ODOL Distortion Tol Trans-Jawa, Feri Selat Sunda & Distorsi ODOL

1,167 km HIGHWAY

Trans-Java Toll Road Jalan Tol Trans-Jawa

Direct Jakarta-Surabaya truck transit slashed to 10–12 hours door-to-door. Coastal shipping takes 28–36 hours plus 16 hours of port handling and double drayage lifts. Trucks capture 85%+ of manufactured cargo. Waktu tempuh truk Jakarta-Surabaya dipangkas menjadi 10–12 jam pintu-ke-pintu. Pelayaran pesisir butuh 28–36 jam plus 16 jam handling dermaga. Truk menguasai 85%+ kargo manufaktur.

HIDDEN SUBSIDY

The ODOL Distortion Distorsi Truk ODOL

Trucks rated for 14 tonnes load 28–35 tonnes (Over-Dimension Over-Load), artificially discounting freight rates by 45%. The hidden cost: IDR 43 trillion/year in asphalt destruction absorbed by taxpayer budgets. Truk berizin 14 ton dijejali 28–35 ton muatan (ODOL), memotong tarif secara semu hingga 45%. Ongkos tersembunyi: kerusakan jalan Rp 43 triliun per tahun ditanggung APBN.

27 km MARITIME BRIDGE

Sunda Strait Ro-Ro Penyeberangan Selat Sunda

30+ Ro-Ro ferries bridge Merak and Bakauheni. Holiday congestion and weather storms cause 15 km truck queues and 24-hour delays. Proposed $20B bridge shelved due to Krakatoa caldera volcanic hazard. 30+ kapal feri Ro-Ro menghubungkan Merak dan Bakauheni. Cuaca buruk memicu antrean truk 15 km dan keterlambatan 24 jam. Jembatan Selat Sunda $20M dibatalkan akibat ancaman vulkanik kaldera Krakatau.
