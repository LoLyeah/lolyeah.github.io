The Total Landed Cost Equation

Freight mode selection is never an isolated fuel comparison. Transporting high-value electronics by water can bankrupt a manufacturer through inventory financing depreciation, while moving iron ore by highway truck wastes millions in rolling friction. Pemilihan moda logistik bukan sekadar perbandingan ongkos bahan bakar. Mengangkut barang elektronik bernilai tinggi via laut dapat membangkrutkan produsen akibat beban bunga modal selama pelayaran, sementara mengangkut bijih besi dengan truk tronton menghamburkan jutaan dolar pada gesekan aspal.

MATHEMATICAL GOVERNING LAW • DOOR-TO-DOOR LANDED EXPENSE HUKUM MATEMATIS TATA KELOLA • BIAYA LOGISTIK DOOR-TO-DOOR
Clanded = (d · m · clinehaul) + (m · Cterminal) + [m · V · (r / 365) · ttransit]
1. Line-Haul Cost: Distance (d), mass (m), and modal propulsion fuel tariff (c). Jarak (d), bobot (m), dan tarif bahan bakar moda (c).
2. Terminal Transfer: Drayage lifts, gantry crane moves, and container yard storage fees. Biaya alih muat crane, drayage truk, dan penumpukan depo.
3. Capital In-Transit: Cargo value (V) tied up under WACC capital interest (r) across dwell days. Nilai kargo (V) terkunci dalam beban bunga modal (r) selama hari transit.

MATHEMATICAL DERIVATION • BREAK-EVEN DISTANCE EQUATION PENURUNAN MATEMATIS • PERSAMAAN JARAK IMPAS
$$d^* = \frac{(C_{\text{terminal, water}} - C_{\text{terminal, truck}}) + V \cdot \frac{r}{8760} \cdot (\text{dwell}_{\text{water}} - \text{dwell}_{\text{truck}})}{(c_{\text{truck}} - c_{\text{water}}) - V \cdot \frac{r}{8760} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{v_{\text{water}}} - \frac{1}{v_{\text{truck}}}\right)}$$

The Inventory Penalty Phenomenon: When cargo value ($V$) increases, the interest financing rate $h = V \cdot \frac{r}{8760}$ grows proportionately. Because water transport moves at 24–30 km/h compared to highway trucking at 75 km/h, the denominator shrinks. If $V$ exceeds the critical threshold ($V > \frac{\Delta c}{\frac{r}{8760} \cdot \Delta(1/v)}$), the denominator turns negative, rendering maritime transit mathematically unviable at any distance. Fenomena Beban Bunga Modal: Saat nilai komoditas ($V$) melonjak tinggi, beban bunga per jam transit $h = V \cdot \frac{r}{8760}$ ikut membesar. Karena kapal berlayar 24–30 km/jam sementara truk melaju 75 km/jam, penyebut rumus menyusut. Jika nilai barang melampaui batas kritis ($V > \frac{\Delta c}{\frac{r}{8760} \cdot \Delta(1/v)}$), penyebut menjadi negatif, membuktikan bahwa angkutan air secara matematis tidak pernah ekonomis pada jarak berapa pun.
