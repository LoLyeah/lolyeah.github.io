The exact distance at which water or rail transport surpasses highway road trucking is determined by equating total landed cost equations: $$d^* = \frac{(C_{\text{terminal, water}} - C_{\text{terminal, truck}}) + V \cdot \frac{r}{365 \cdot 24} \cdot (\text{dwell}_{\text{water}} - \text{dwell}_{\text{truck}})}{(c_{\text{truck}} - c_{\text{water}}) - V \cdot \frac{r}{365 \cdot 24} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{v_{\text{water}}} - \frac{1}{v_{\text{truck}}}\right)}$$ When cargo value ($V$) increases, the inventory holding penalty per hour of slower transit shrinks the fuel savings denominator. Above critical threshold values, water is mathematically never viable.

Jarak tepat di mana angkutan air atau kereta api mulai lebih murah dibanding truk jalan raya ditentukan dengan menyamakan fungsi biaya total: $$d^* = \frac{(C_{\text{terminal, air}} - C_{\text{terminal, truk}}) + V \cdot \frac{r}{365 \cdot 24} \cdot (\text{dwell}_{\text{air}} - \text{dwell}_{\text{truk}})}{(c_{\text{truk}} - c_{\text{air}}) - V \cdot \frac{r}{365 \cdot 24} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{v_{\text{air}}} - \frac{1}{v_{\text{truk}}}\right)}$$ Ketika nilai kargo ($V$) melonjak tinggi, beban bunga barang selama pelayaran yang lambat mengikis selisih hemat solar di penyebut rumus. Di atas nilai ambang kritis tertentu, transportasi air secara matematis tidak pernah ekonomis.
