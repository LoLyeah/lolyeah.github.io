Physics & Thermodynamics of Freight: The Buoyant Law

Fisika & Termodinamika Angkutan Kargo: Hukum Daya Apung

Freight efficiency begins at the molecular interface between cargo mass and the Earth's gravitational field. Moving matter horizontally requires work against two fundamental resistive regimes: hydrostatic fluid displacement and solid-state contact deformation.

Efisiensi logistik berakar pada antarmuka molekuler antara massa muatan dan gravitasi bumi. Memindahkan benda secara horizontal menuntut kerja mekanik melawan dua rezim hambatan fundamental: pemindahan fluida hidrostatik dan deformasi kontak zat padat.

ARCHIMEDES HYDROSTATIC EQUILIBRIUM
F_b = ρ × g × ∇ = m_total × g

A cargo ship uses zero engine power to suspend its mass above the earth's center. Karena densitas air laut mencapai $\approx 1.025\text{ kg/m}^3$, memindahkan 220.000 meter kubik air laut menghasilkan gaya angkat apung 2.210 megaNewton. Kapal laut membutuhkan nol daya mesin untuk menahan bobotnya terhadap gravitasi bumi.

Hydrodynamic Shear & Wave-Making Drag

Gesekan Geser Hidrodinamika & Gelombang Haluan

Total hydrodynamic resistance ($R_T$) decomposes into frictional shear resistance ($R_F$) along the submerged wetted surface ($S$) and wave-making pressure resistance ($R_W$).

Total tahanan hidrodinamika kapal ($R_T$) terbagi menjadi tahanan gesekan geser ($R_F$) di sepanjang permukaan lambung basah ($S$) dan tahanan gelombang haluan ($R_W$).

ITTC-1957 SKIN FRICTION CORRELATION
C_F = 0.075 / [log_10(Re) - 2]^2
At Reynolds number $Re \approx 2.5 \times 10^9$, $C_F \approx 0.00138$. Friction per unit area drops exponentially at large vessel scales.
FROUDE NUMBER & WAVE RESISTANCE

Rolling Hysteresis & Steel Rail Contact

Histeresis Roda Karet & Kontak Rel Baja

PNEUMATIC RUBBER ON ASPHALT
C_rr ≈ 0.0070 - 0.0090

Pneumatic truck tires flex upon the roadbed. The molecular polymer chains undergo cyclic deformation, dissipating heat through viscoelastic damping. A 40-tonne semi-truck continuously burns 75 kW purely deforming its own rubber. Ban truk karet tertekan dan meregang di atas aspal. Rantai polimer mengalami deformasi siklik yang membuang energi menjadi panas. Truk 40 ton membuang 75 kW daya mesin solar hanya untuk melenturkan karet bannya sendiri.

HARDENED STEEL WHEEL ON STEEL RAIL
C_rr ≈ 0.0015 - 0.0020

Hardened steel wheels contacting steel rails produce micro-scale contact patches with virtually zero hysteretic damping. Freight rail achieves 4 times less rolling resistance than highway trucking. Roda baja berkekerasan tinggi pada rel baja hanya menghasilkan deformasi mikroskopis tanpa histeresis karet. Kereta api kargo memiliki tahanan gelinding 4 kali lebih rendah dibanding truk jalan raya.
