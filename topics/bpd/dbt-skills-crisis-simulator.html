Acute Distress Protocol Protokol Distres Akut

The T.I.P.P. Crisis Survival Simulator Simulator Bertahan Krisis T.I.P.P.

Direct physiological intervention to hack the autonomic nervous system when Subjective Units of Distress (SUDS) exceed 80/100. Intervensi fisiologis langsung untuk mengintervensi sistem saraf otonom saat skala distres emosi (SUDS) melampaui 80/100.

T Cold Water

Temperature (Dive Reflex) Suhu (Mammalian Dive Reflex)

Submerging face in a bowl of cold water (10-15°C) while holding breath for 15-30s stimulates trigeminal receptors, firing the vagus nerve and triggering acute parasympathetic bradycardia (dropping heart rate by 10-25 bpm). Membenamkan wajah ke mangkuk air dingin (10-15°C) sambil menahan napas 15-30 detik merangsang saraf trigeminal, mengaktifkan saraf vagus, dan memicu bradikardia parasimpatis yang langsung menurunkan detak jantung 10-25 bpm.

I 20 Min Burst

Intense Exercise Latihan Fisik Intens

20-30 minutes of high-intensity aerobic activity (sprinting, jumping jacks, fast stair climbing) metabolizes the surge of systemic adrenaline and cortisol, exhausting sympathetic fight-or-flight energy safely. 20-30 menit aktivitas aerobik intensitas tinggi (lari cepat, jumping jacks, naik tangga cepat) membakar lonjakan hormon adrenalin dan kortisol sistemik, menguras energi 'lawan-atau-lari' secara aman.

P 4s In / 7s Out

Paced Breathing Pernapasan Teratur

Breathing deeply from the diaphragm: 4-second inhale, followed by a prolonged 7-second exhale. Prolonged exhalation increases cardiac vagal tone, stimulating acetylcholine release to slow heart rate. Bernapas dalam dari diafragma: tarik napas 4 detik, lalu hembuskan perlahan selama 7 detik. Hembusan yang lebih panjang meningkatkan tonus vagal jantung dan merilis asetilkolin untuk meredakan ketegangan.

P Tension-Release

Paired Muscle Relaxation Relaksasi Otot Berpasangan

Inhale and tense muscle groups (fists, shoulders, calves) with 100% effort for 5-7 seconds, then exhale completely, letting muscles drop slack while silently saying "Relax". Tarik napas dan kencangkan kelompok otot (tinju, bahu, betis) sekuat tenaga selama 5-7 detik, lalu hembuskan napas tuntas sambil melemaskan otot sepenuhnya dan mengucap "Tenang" dalam hati.

Parasympathetic Reset Trainer Latihan Reset Saraf Parasimpatis

Live 4-Second Inhale / 7-Second Exhale Vagal Pacer Pemandu Napas Vagal 4 Detik Tarik / 7 Detik Hembus

Ready --

Follow the expanding and shrinking circle. Inhale through your nose into your belly for 4 seconds, brief pause, then exhale slowly through pursed lips for 7 seconds. Ikuti lingkaran yang membesar dan mengecil. Tarik napas lewat hidung ke perut selama 4 detik, jeda singkat, lalu hembuskan perlahan lewat bibir selama 7 detik.

Interpersonal Effectiveness Module Modul Efektivitas Interpersonal

Interactive D.E.A.R. M.A.N. Assertiveness Generator Generator Naskah Asertif D.E.A.R. M.A.N.

Construct structured, non-confrontational boundary requests without falling into splitting, passive-aggressive withdrawal, or explosive rage. Susun permintaan batasan yang terstruktur dan non-konfrontatif tanpa terjebak splitting, penarikan diri dingin, atau ledakan amarah.

Remember the "M.A.N." Delivery Principles:
  • M (Mindful): Stay focused on your goal; do not get distracted by counter-attacks ("Broken Record technique").
  • A (Appear Confident): Keep eye contact, steady voice, and relaxed posture (avoid apologizing for having needs).
  • N (Negotiate): Be willing to give a little to get what matters; ask "What do you think would work?"
