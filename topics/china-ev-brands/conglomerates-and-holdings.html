The Era of "Reverse Technology Licensing"

Era Baru: Lisensi Teknologi Terbalik (Reverse Technology Licensing)

For 40 years (1984–2022), foreign automakers entered China via mandatory 50:50 JVs to teach manufacturing to Chinese partners. Today, the dynamic has reversed: European and Japanese legacy OEMs are buying equity and licensing EV/software architectures from Chinese automakers.

Selama 40 tahun (1984–2022), pabrikan asing masuk ke Tiongkok melalui aturan wajib JV 50:50. Kini dinamika telah berbalik: pabrikan legendaris Eropa dan Jepang membeli saham dan melisensikan platform EV serta sistem perangkat lunak dari produsen Tiongkok.
