Engineering BreakthroughsTerobosan Rekayasa Teknologi

Technological Moats Defining Modern Chinese EVs

Pilar Keunggulan Teknologi Rekayasa Mobil Listrik Tiongkok

From megawatt-rate charging and solid-state chemistries to mega-diecasting and end-to-end neural world models.

Dari pengisian kilat daya tinggi dan kimia baterai solid-state hingga super die-casting dan model neural world AI.

5.5C

800V–900V SiC, BYD Flash Charge & 5.5C Cells

Arsitektur SiC 800V–900V, BYD Flash Charge & Sel 5.5C

High-voltage Silicon Carbide power stages combined with 5.5C–6C ultra-fast charging (BYD e-Platform 3.0 Evo, CATL Shenxing Plus, Zeekr Golden Battery) replenish 400 to 550 km of range in 10.5 minutes at 500+ kW peak rates, backed by dual-gun fast charging and low-temperature pulse self-heating.

Arsitektur semikonduktor Silicon Carbide (SiC) dipadukan sel ultra-fast 5.5C–6C (BYD e-Platform 3.0 Evo, CATL Shenxing Plus, Zeekr Golden Battery) mengisi 400–550 km jarak tempuh dalam 10,5 menit pada daya puncak 500+ kW, didukung teknologi pengisian ganda (dual-gun) dan pemanasan denyut suhu rendah.

SSB

Semi-Solid & Solid-State Batteries (1,000+ km)

Baterai Semi-Solid & All-Solid-State (Jarak 1.000+ km)

Commercialized 150 kWh semi-solid-state packs (Nio, IM Motors Lightyear) achieve 360 Wh/kg energy density for over 1,000 km of real-world endurance. Major OEMs (GAC, BYD, Chery, CATL) have roadmap timelines for 2026–2027 all-solid-state volume production with zero thermal runaway risk.

Paket baterai semi-solid-state 150 kWh (Nio, IM Motors Lightyear) mencapai kepadatan energi 360 Wh/kg untuk jarak tempuh riil 1.000+ km. Pabrikan utama (GAC, BYD, Chery, CATL) menjadwalkan komersialisasi massal all-solid-state pada 2026–2027 dengan ketahanan suhu ekstrem tanpa risiko kebakaran.

CTB

9,000t+ Giga-Casting & Cell-to-Body (CTB)

Super Die-Casting 9.000t+ & Integrasi Bodi CTB

Giant giga-casting presses (Xiaomi 9,100t Hyper Die-Casting, Geely 9,000t, Seres) cast entire rear sub-assemblies in single operations—eliminating 800+ weld joints and cutting 15% structural weight while elevating torsional stiffness beyond 45,000–51,000 Nm/deg alongside structural battery floors.

Mesin cetak raksasa (Xiaomi 9.100t Hyper Die-Casting, Geely 9.000t, Seres) mencetak seluruh rangka lantai belakang dalam satu proses tunggal—menghilangkan 800+ titik las dan memangkas 15% bobot, sekaligus mendongkrak kekakuan bodi di atas 45.000–51.000 Nm/derajat bersama lantai baterai struktural CTB.

AI

End-to-End VLA Neural Autonomous Driving

Kemudi Otonom AI Model Neural End-to-End (VLA)

Huawei Qiankun ADS 3.0, XPeng XNGP, and Xiaomi HAD deploy multimodal Vision-Language-Action (VLA) neural networks with LiDAR and vision fusion. Eliminating brittle rules and HD map dependence allows true parking-space to parking-space autonomous navigation across complex urban environments.

Huawei Qiankun ADS 3.0, XPeng XNGP, dan Xiaomi HAD menerapkan arsitektur neural Vision-Language-Action (VLA) multimodal dengan fusi LiDAR. Menghapus ketergantungan aturan manual dan peta HD memungkinkan mobil bernavigasi mandiri dari titik parkir awal ke tujuan akhir di perkotaan padat.

e⁴

Fully Active Digital Chassis & 4-Motor Vectoring

Sasis Aktif Cerdas & Torque Vectoring 4 Motor

Full-active hydraulic and electromagnetic suspension systems (BYD DiSus-P/X, Nio SkyRide steer-by-wire, Huawei Tuling) continuously adapt to road topography via LiDAR preview. Paired with 4 independent in-wheel motors (BYD e⁴), vehicles perform 360° tank turns, tire-blowout stabilization, and emergency water floating.

Suspensi hidrolik dan elektromagnetik aktif penuh (BYD DiSus-P/X, Nio SkyRide steer-by-wire, Huawei Tuling) beradaptasi dengan kontur jalan via pemindaian LiDAR. Didukung 4 motor roda independen (BYD e⁴), kendaraan dapat melakukan tank-turn 360°, manuver stabil saat ban pecah, hingga mengapung darurat di air.

DM-i

>46% Thermal Efficiency Hybrids & 2,100km EREV

Super Hybrid Efisiensi >46% & EREV 2.100 km

Fifth-generation dedicated hybrid and range-extender powertrains (BYD DM-i 5.0, Geely NordThor EM-i, Chery Kunpeng C-DM) exceed 46.5% thermal efficiency benchmarks, unlocking real-world fuel consumption under 2.9 L/100 km and continuous cross-country driving ranges spanning 2,100 km to 2,500 km.

Powertrain hybrid dan extended-range generasi ke-5 (BYD DM-i 5.0, Geely NordThor EM-i, Chery Kunpeng C-DM) menembus efisiensi termal 46,5%, menghasilkan konsumsi BBM di bawah 2,9 L/100 km dan jarak jelajah nonstop antar-provinsi mencapai 2.100 km hingga 2.500 km.
