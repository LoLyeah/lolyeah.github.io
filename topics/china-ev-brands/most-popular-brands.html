Technological Moats Defining Modern Chinese EVs

Pilar Teknologi Kunci yang Mengunggulkan Mobil Listrik Tiongkok

800V

High-Voltage SiC & 5C/6C Ultra Charging

Arsitektur SiC 800V–900V & Fast Charging 5C/6C

Transitioning from 400V to 800V/900V Silicon Carbide systems allows 300 to 500 km of range to be replenished in 10 to 12 minutes, eliminating charging anxiety on long-distance road trips.

Transisi ke sistem Silicon Carbide 800V/900V memungkinkan pengisian daya 300 hingga 500 km dalam waktu 10–12 menit, menghapus rasa cemas pengisian daya saat perjalanan antarkota.

CTB

Cell-to-Body (CTB) & Structural Packs

Integrasi Struktural Cell-to-Body (CTB) & CTC

Integrating battery cells directly into the vehicle floor as structural members boosts vehicle torsional stiffness past 40,000 Nm/deg while dramatically reducing interior floor height and weight.

Penyatuan sel baterai langsung ke rangka bodi mobil sebagai elemen struktural melipatgandakan kekakuan bodi di atas 40.000 Nm/derajat sekaligus menurunkan lantai kabin dan bobot mobil.

AI

End-to-End AI Autonomous Driving

Sistem Kemudi Otonom AI End-to-End

Huawei Qiankun ADS 3.0, XPeng XNGP, and Xiaomi HAD deploy end-to-end neural network models with LiDAR and vision fusion, allowing door-to-door navigation without reliance on high-definition maps.

Huawei ADS 3.0, XPeng XNGP, dan Xiaomi HAD menerapkan arsitektur model neural network end-to-end tanpa peta HD, memungkinkan mobil bermanuver mandiri dari tempat parkir ke tujuan akhir.

e⁴

Quad-Motor Torque Vectoring & Active Susses

Torque Vectoring 4 Motor & Sasis Aktif Cerdas

Dedicated four-motor independent torque vectoring (BYD e⁴) and active hydraulic suspension (DiSus, SkyRide) enable instantaneous millimeter-level body stabilization, tire-burst safety, and 360-degree tank turns.

Sistem motor independen 4 roda (BYD e⁴) dan suspensi hidrolik aktif (DiSus, SkyRide) memungkinkan stabilisasi sasis dalam hitungan milidetik, kendali saat ban pecah, hingga tank-turn 360 derajat.
