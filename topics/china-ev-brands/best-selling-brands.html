The EREV Revolution: Why Extended Range Won Family Buyers

Revolusi EREV: Alasan Mesin Range Extender Memikat Keluarga

Extended Range Electric Vehicles (EREV) pioneered by Li Auto and rapidly adopted by Deepal, AITO, Voyah, and Leapmotor have surged to over 40% of the premium NEV market. By delivering 200–300 km of daily pure-electric city range with an onboard generator that extends highway trips to 1,300+ km, EREVs completely bypass holiday charger congestion while maintaining smooth electric motor torque.

Kendaraan Extended Range (EREV) yang dipelopori Li Auto dan diadopsi cepat oleh Deepal, AITO, Voyah, serta Leapmotor kini menyumbang lebih dari 40% segmen NEV premium. Memberikan 200–300 km jarak listrik murni di dalam kota serta generator bensin untuk perjalanan jauh hingga 1.300+ km, EREV membebaskan pengemudi dari antrean panjang SPKLU di hari libur.

Price Elasticity & The 79,800 RMB Inflection Point

Elastisitas Harga & Titik Balik 79.800 RMB

In early 2024, BYD launched its "Honor Editions", pricing the Qin Plus DM-i PHEV at just 79,800 RMB (~$11,000 USD). This declared "Electricity cheaper than oil", causing an irreversible mass defection from legacy Japanese and German compact sedans (Corolla, Sylphy, Lavida) to Chinese domestic plug-ins, permanently shifting domestic market share above 84%.

Pada awal 2024, peluncuran varian "Honor Edition" oleh BYD menetapkan harga sedan hybrid Qin Plus DM-i di angka 79.800 RMB (~Rp 175 juta). Slogan "Listrik Lebih Murah dari Minyak" ini memicu perpindahan konsumen massal dari sedan Jepang/Jerman (Corolla, Sylphy, Lavida) ke mobil plug-in lokal, mengerek pangsa pasar merek Tiongkok melampaui 84%.
