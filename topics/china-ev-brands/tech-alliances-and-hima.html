Huawei HIMA Alliance & Big Tech Mobility Aliansi Huawei HIMA & Raksasa Teknologi Otomotif

How consumer tech giants redefined China's automotive industry. Explore the 5 'Jie' (五界) realms of the Huawei HIMA alliance (AITO, Luxeed, Stelato, Maextro, Shangjie), the breakout success of Xiaomi Auto's HyperOS ecosystem, and Baidu's Apollo AI integration.

Bagaimana raksasa teknologi mendefinisikan ulang industri mobil listrik di Tiongkok. Bedah tuntas 5 aliansi cerdas Huawei HIMA (AITO, Luxeed, Stelato, Maextro, Shangjie), kesuksesan ekosistem HyperOS Xiaomi Auto, serta integrasi AI Baidu Apollo.

Huawei HIMA (Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance) 鸿蒙智行
THE 5 REALMS ALLIANCE (五界)

Under the HIMA model, Huawei does not build cars directly; instead, it provides end-to-end vehicle definitions, exterior/interior industrial styling, chipsets, operating systems (HarmonyOS Cockpit), autonomous driving (Qiankun ADS 3.0), DriveONE powertrains, and sells vehicles through its vast consumer retail store network.

Dalam model HIMA, Huawei tidak memproduksi mobil sendiri secara mandiri, melainkan mendefinisikan desain produk, sistem operasi (HarmonyOS Cockpit), sistem kemudi otonom cerdas (Qiankun ADS 3.0), motor listrik DriveONE, serta menjualnya di ribuan toko ritel Huawei.

LUXURY SUV PILLAR

AITO (问界)

Partner OEM: Seres Group (赛力斯)

The flagship volume pillar of HIMA. The AITO M9 is China's #1 selling luxury SUV above ¥500k, joined by the high-volume M7 family crossover and M5 sports SUV.

Pilar volume utama HIMA. AITO M9 merupakan SUV mewah terlaris di Tiongkok di atas 500.000 RMB, ditemani SUV keluarga M7 dan crossover M5.

Key Models: AITO M9, AITO M8, AITO M7, AITO M5
LUXEED
DYNAMIC COUPE & SUV

Luxeed (智界)

Partner OEM: Chery Holding (奇瑞)

Targets youthful performance and digital aerodynamics. The S7 electric sports sedan (0.203 Cd) and R7 coupe SUV combine Chery's E0X engineering with Huawei ADS 3.0.

Menargetkan pasar anak muda dan aerodinamika digital. Sedan sport S7 (0.203 Cd) dan coupe SUV R7 memadukan sasis Chery E0X dan Huawei ADS 3.0.

Key Models: Luxeed S7, Luxeed R7
EXECUTIVE LUXURY

Stelato (享界)

Partner OEM: BAIC BluePark (北汽蓝谷)

Executive luxury limousine brand. The Stelato S9 features zero-gravity second-row captain chairs, laser projection rear cinema, and 816 km 800V CLTC range.

Merek sedan eksekutif mewah. Stelato S9 menawarkan kursi zero-gravity baris kedua, proyektor bioskop laser, dan jarak tempuh 816 km 800V CLTC.

Key Models: Stelato S9 Executive Flagship
MAEXTRO
ULTRA-LUXURY HYPER

Maextro (尊界)

Partner OEM: JAC Group (江淮汽车)

Ultra-luxury million-RMB flagship brand targeting the pinnacle sovereign bracket (Rolls-Royce Ghost and Mercedes-Maybach S-Class tier).

Merek kemewahan puncak bernilai 1 juta+ RMB yang menyaingi kelas kasta tertinggi dunia seperti Mercedes-Maybach S-Class dan Rolls-Royce Ghost.

Key Models: Maextro S800 Sovereign Limousine
MAINSTREAM ACCESSIBLE

Shangjie (尚界)

Partner OEM: SAIC Motor (上汽集团)

The newest 5th realm announced in late 2025/2026 with SAIC. Aimed at bringing Huawei's intelligent cockpit and Qiankun smart driving to mass mainstream family segments.

Aliansi ke-5 terbaru bersama SAIC Motor untuk menghadirkan kokpit cerdas dan kemampuan kemudi cerdas Qiankun ke segmen keluarga yang lebih luas.

Key Models: Shangjie H5 & Mainstream EV SUVs

HarmonyOS Cockpit Seamless smartphone/tablet mirroring, 3D spatial audio, and multimodal AI voice assistant.

Xiaomi Auto: The Smartphone-to-Car Miracle

Xiaomi Auto: Sinergi Ekosistem Ponsel ke Mobil Listrik

Xiaomi's $10 billion foray into automaking created an immediate consumer sensation. By leveraging its vast supply chain, 600M+ active HyperOS device user base, and retail store network, Xiaomi turned the SU7 into a lifestyle statement.

Investasi 10 miliar dolar Xiaomi menciptakan sensasi instan di pasar otomotif. Memanfaatkan rantai pasok global, 600 juta pengguna ekosistem HyperOS, dan jaringan toko ritel, Xiaomi menjadikan SU7 sebagai ikon gaya hidup modern.

  • HyperOS Connectivity: Instant magnetic tablet docking, automatic phone mirroring, and seamless home appliance control from the steering wheel.
  • Hyper Die-Casting: 9,100-ton integrated die-casting cluster reducing vehicle rear floor part count by 72 components.
  • Ultra-High RPM Motors: In-house developed HyperEngine V6, V6s, and V8s spinning at up to 27,200 rpm.

Baidu Apollo, JiYue & CATL Ecosystem

Baidu Apollo, JiYue & Peran Kunci Baterai CATL

Baidu collaborated with Geely to create JiYue Auto (极越), pioneering AI-native cars with purely vision-based autonomous driving (PPA) and large language model (LLM) voice control. Meanwhile, CATL acts as the technological backbone powering over 40% of the entire Chinese EV ecosystem.

Baidu bermitra dengan Geely melahirkan JiYue Auto (极越), memelopori mobil AI dengan sistem kemudi otonom berbasis visi murni (PPA) dan LLM. Sementara itu, CATL menjadi tulang punggung baterai bagi lebih dari 40% seluruh ekosistem EV Tiongkok.

  • CATL Qilin & Shenxing: 1,000+ km range battery packs and 4C/5C ultra-fast LFP charging cells powering Li Auto, Avatr, Zeekr, and Xiaomi.
  • Baidu Apollo Autonomous: Millions of test kilometers in commercial robotaxis deployed across Wuhan, Beijing, and Guangzhou.
