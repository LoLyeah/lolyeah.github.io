How consumer tech giants redefined China's automotive industry. Explore the 5 'Jie' (五界) realms of the Huawei HIMA alliance (AITO, Luxeed, Stelato, Maextro, Shangjie), the breakout success of Xiaomi Auto's HyperOS ecosystem, and Baidu's Apollo AI integration.

Bagaimana raksasa teknologi mendefinisikan ulang industri mobil listrik di Tiongkok. Bedah tuntas 5 aliansi cerdas Huawei HIMA (AITO, Luxeed, Stelato, Maextro, Shangjie), kesuksesan ekosistem HyperOS Xiaomi Auto, serta integrasi AI Baidu Apollo.

Huawei HIMA (Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance) 鸿蒙智行
THE 5 REALMS ALLIANCE (五界)

Under the HIMA model, Huawei does not build cars directly; instead, it provides end-to-end vehicle definitions, exterior/interior industrial styling, chipsets, operating systems (HarmonyOS Cockpit), autonomous driving (Qiankun ADS 3.0), DriveONE powertrains, and sells vehicles through its vast consumer retail store network.

Dalam model HIMA, Huawei tidak memproduksi mobil sendiri secara mandiri, melainkan mendefinisikan desain produk, sistem operasi (HarmonyOS Cockpit), sistem kemudi otonom cerdas (Qiankun ADS 3.0), motor listrik DriveONE, serta menjualnya di ribuan toko ritel Huawei.

LUXURY SUV PILLAR

AITO (问界)

Partner OEM: Seres Group (赛力斯) • Yinwang Stakeholder

The flagship volume pillar of HIMA. The AITO M9 is China's #1 selling luxury vehicle above ¥500k (180,000+ units delivered), joined by the all-new M8 executive 6-seater, M7 Ultra family SUV, and M5 sports crossover.

Pilar volume utama HIMA. AITO M9 adalah mobil mewah terlaris di Tiongkok di atas 500.000 RMB (>180.000 unit terkirim), didampingi SUV eksekutif 6-seater M8 terbaru, M7 Ultra, dan crossover sport M5.

Key Models: AITO M9 (BEV/EREV), AITO M8, AITO M7 Ultra, AITO M5
DYNAMIC COUPE & SUV

Luxeed (智界)

Partner OEM: Chery Holding (奇瑞集团)

Youthful dynamic performance and aero styling. The breakout Luxeed R7 fastback coupe SUV recorded over 30,000 orders in its first month, complementing the S7 electric sports sedan (0.203 Cd) on Chery's 800V E0X platform.

Menargetkan performa dinamis dan aerodinamika digital. SUV coupe fastback Luxeed R7 membukukan lebih dari 30.000 pesanan di bulan pertama, melengkapi sedan sport listrik S7 (0.203 Cd) pada platform Chery 800V E0X.

Key Models: Luxeed R7 Fastback SUV, Luxeed S7 Sports Sedan
EXECUTIVE LUXURY

Stelato (享界)

Partner OEM: BAIC BluePark (北汽蓝谷)

Executive luxury limousine realm. The Stelato S9 features zero-gravity second-row presidential captain chairs, 32-inch laser projection rear theater, 816 km 800V CLTC range, and 4-wheel active steering.

Merek sedan eksekutif mewah kasta kepresidenan. Stelato S9 menawarkan kursi zero-gravity baris kedua, proyektor bioskop laser 32 inci, jarak tempuh 816 km 800V CLTC, dan kemudi 4 roda aktif.

Key Models: Stelato S9 Executive Flagship, Stelato S9 Touring
ULTRA-LUXURY HYPER

Maextro (尊界)

Partner OEM: JAC Group (江淮汽车)

The pinnacle ¥1M–¥1.5M RMB sovereign luxury realm. The Maextro S800 is a 5.48-meter flagship sovereign limousine equipped with dual-tone starry ceiling, L3-ready Qiankun ADS 3.0, and active steer-by-wire hydraulic suspension.

Merek kemewahan puncak bernilai 1–1,5 Juta RMB. Maextro S800 adalah limousine megah sepanjang 5,48 meter dengan plafon langit berbintang, Qiankun ADS 3.0 siap otonom L3, dan suspensi hidrolik steer-by-wire aktif.

Key Models: Maextro S800 Sovereign Limousine
MAINSTREAM ACCESSIBLE

Shangjie (尚界)

Partner OEM: SAIC Motor (上汽集团)

The 5th realm co-created with SAIC Motor. Engineered to bring Huawei's intelligent cockpit and Qiankun smart driving suite to high-volume mainstream family EV and crossover segments.

Aliansi ke-5 yang dibentuk bersama SAIC Motor. Dirancang untuk menghadirkan kokpit cerdas HarmonyOS dan kemudi pintar Qiankun ke segmen EV dan crossover keluarga massal.

Key Models: Shangjie H5 & Mainstream Family EV SUVs
Qiankun ADS 3.0 End-to-End neural network network with GOD & PDP architecture for map-free city driving and valet parking.
HarmonyOS Cockpit 4 Seamless smartphone/tablet mirroring, 3D spatial audio, Celia multimodal LLM, and satellite messaging.
Tuling Smart Chassis Multi-modal road perception preview scanning 1,000x/sec with active air suspension and torque vectoring.
DriveONE 800V SiC Ultra-compact high-rpm electric motor units with up to 25,000 rpm, 5C charging, and 98% inverter efficiency.

Xiaomi Auto: The "Human × Car × Home" Ecosystem

Xiaomi Auto: Ekosistem "Human × Car × Home"

Xiaomi's automotive disruption has delivered over 135,000 vehicles in its first operational year. Leveraging its 600M+ active HyperOS device user base, 9,100t Hyper Die-Casting, and retail network, Xiaomi expanded from the SU7 sedan to the 1,548 hp SU7 Ultra and the all-new 2026 YU7 luxury electric SUV.

Disrupsi otomotif Xiaomi berhasil mengirimkan lebih dari 135.000 mobil di tahun pertamanya. Memanfaatkan 600 juta+ pengguna ekosistem HyperOS, mesin cetak 9.100t Hyper Die-Casting, dan jaringan toko ritel, Xiaomi berekspansi dari sedan SU7 ke monster 1.548 hp SU7 Ultra dan SUV mewah YU7 terbaru 2026.

  • HyperOS Connectivity: Instant magnetic tablet docking, automatic phone mirroring, and seamless home appliance control from the steering wheel.
  • Hyper Die-Casting: 9,100-ton integrated die-casting cluster reducing vehicle rear floor part count by 72 components.
  • HyperEngine Ultra Motors: In-house developed V6s and V8s motors spinning up to 27,200 rpm, generating 1,548 hp on the SU7 Ultra.

XPeng AI, Baidu Apollo & CATL Powerhouse

XPeng AI, Baidu Apollo & Tulang Punggung Baterai CATL

Tech titans continue redefining the smart vehicle stack: XPeng launched the P7+ as the world's first AI-defined vehicle alongside the blockbuster Mona M03. Baidu Apollo powers JiYue's pure-vision AI driving and autonomous robotaxis, while CATL anchors over 40% of global EV supply.

Raksasa teknologi terus mendefinisikan ulang mobil cerdas: XPeng meluncurkan P7+ sebagai mobil AI pertama di dunia bersama sedan fenomenal Mona M03. Baidu Apollo mentenagai JiYue berbasis visi murni dan robotaxi otonom, sementara CATL memasok lebih dari 40% baterai EV global.

  • CATL Freevoy & Shenxing Plus: 400+ km range EREV super-hybrid batteries with 4C charging and 1,000 km 5C LFP battery packs.
  • XPeng AI Hawkeye & Turing Chip: Pure-vision end-to-end VLA autonomous driving with in-house 40-core neural processing silicon.
  • Baidu Apollo Autonomous: Millions of commercial driverless robotaxi miles operating across Wuhan, Beijing, and Shanghai.
