The BYD Atto 1 Phenomenon: Overtaking the LCGC Market

Fenomena BYD Atto 1: Melibas Pasar LCGC Nasional

Launched in mid-2025 with an aggressive starting price of Rp 199 Million (OTR Jakarta), the BYD Atto 1 delivered 22,582 units in 2025 and surged to over 4,233 units/month in 2026. Equipped with a 30.08–38.88 kWh Blade Battery (300–380 km range) and modern cockpit tech, it directly dismantled the entry-level gasoline LCGC monopoly (Toyota Agya, Daihatsu Ayla, Honda Brio).

Diluncurkan pertengahan 2025 dengan harga mulai Rp 199 Juta (OTR Jakarta), BYD Atto 1 mencatatkan penjualan 22.582 unit di 2025 dan melesat hingga 4.233 unit/bulan di 2026. Berbekal Blade Battery 30,08–38,88 kWh (jarak tempuh 300–380 km) dan fitur modern, Atto 1 sukses menggerus dominasi mobil LCGC bensin konvensional.

The BYD M6 Breakthrough: 7-Seater Family Mobility

Terobosan BYD M6: Menaklukkan Budaya MPV 7-Seater

Indonesia is an MPV-centric market where 7-seater vehicles represent the core family transport. The BYD M6 recorded 10,682 deliveries in 2025, offering a true 3-row 7-seater MPV layout with up to 530 km range and Blade battery safety at Rp 379M – Rp 429M, directly challenging conventional gasoline MPVs like the Toyota Innova Zenix and Avanza.

Indonesia adalah pasar berorientasi MPV di mana mobil 7-penumpang menjadi tulang punggung mobilitas keluarga. BYD M6 membukukan 10.682 pengiriman di 2025, menghadirkan MPV 3 baris 7-penumpang dengan jarak tempuh hingga 530 km dan Blade Battery seharga Rp 379M – Rp 429M yang menantang langsung Innova Zenix bensin.

Perpres 79/2023 & Tax Holiday Framework

Perpres 79/2023 & Insentif Bebas Bea Masuk

Under Presidential Regulation No. 79/2023 and Permeninvest No. 6/2023, automakers committing to build local factories in Indonesia receive 0% import duty and 0% luxury sales tax (PPnBM) on CBU units during their factory construction period.

Melalui Perpres No. 79/2023 dan Permeninvest No. 6/2023, pabrikan yang berkomitmen membangun pabrik di Indonesia diberikan insentif bebas bea masuk impor dan PPnBM 0% untuk mobil CBU selama masa konstruksi pabrik.

TKDN & 1% PPN Value-Added Tax

Syarat TKDN & Insentif PPN 1 Persen

Electric vehicles assembled in Indonesia with a Domestic Component Level (TKDN) of at least 40% receive a government VAT subsidy (PPN DTP), cutting the buyer's VAT from the standard rate down to just 1%, drastically lowering purchase prices.

Kendaraan listrik yang dirakit di Indonesia dengan Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri (TKDN) minimal 40% berhak memperoleh insentif PPN Ditanggung Pemerintah (PPN DTP), sehingga pembeli hanya membayar PPN 1% dari tarif standar.

Nickel Downstream & HLI Karawang

Hilirisasi Nikel & Pabrik Sel Baterai HLI

Indonesia's nickel downstreaming fostered a complete supply chain from HPAL nickel processing in Morowali/Weda Bay to 10 GWh battery cell factories (PT HLI Green Power Karawang and Gotion High-Tech), creating an integrated Southeast Asian EV manufacturing base.

Kebijakan hilirisasi nikel mendorong ekosistem lengkap dari pengolahan HPAL di Morowali/Weda Bay hingga pabrik sel baterai 10 GWh di Karawang (PT HLI Green Power) dan Gotion High-Tech, menjadikan Indonesia pusat manufaktur EV terintegrasi di ASEAN.
