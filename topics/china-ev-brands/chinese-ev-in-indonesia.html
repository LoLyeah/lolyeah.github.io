The 7-Seater MPV Breakthrough: BYD M6 & Denza D9

Terobosan MPV 7-Penumpang: Fenomena BYD M6 & Denza D9

Indonesia is fundamentally an MPV-dominated car market, where 7-seater vehicles (Toyota Avanza, Innova, Alphard) represent the backbone of personal transport. Prior to 2024, nearly all affordable EVs were small hatchbacks (Air ev, BinguoEV) or 5-seater crossovers, limiting their utility for multi-generational families.

The launch of the BYD M6 at GIIAS 2024 priced between Rp 379M and Rp 429M broke this barrier, offering a genuine 3-row, 7-seater electric MPV with 530 km NEDC range and Blade battery safety at the exact price point of conventional ICE compact MPVs.

Indonesia adalah pasar otomotif yang didominasi budaya MPV, di mana mobil 7-penumpang (Toyota Avanza, Innova, Alphard) menjadi tulang punggung mobilitas keluarga. Sebelum 2024, mayoritas EV terjangkau berbentuk hatchback mini atau crossover 5-seater yang kurang cocok untuk keluarga besar.

Peluncuran BYD M6 di GIIAS 2024 dengan banderol Rp 379 Juta – Rp 429 Juta mendisrupsi pasar secara telak: menghadirkan MPV listrik 7-seater dengan jarak tempuh hingga 530 km NEDC dan Blade Battery di rentang harga mobil bensin konvensional.

Perpres 79/2023 & Tax Holiday Incentives

Perpres 79/2023 & Insentif Bebas Bea Masuk

Under Presidential Regulation No. 79/2023 and Permeninvest No. 6/2023, automakers committing to build local factories in Indonesia can import CBU electric vehicles with 0% import duty and 0% luxury tax (PPnBM) during their factory construction phase.

Melalui Perpres No. 79/2023 dan Permeninvest No. 6/2023, pabrikan yang berkomitmen membangun pabrik di Indonesia diberikan insentif bebas bea masuk impor dan PPnBM 0% untuk mobil CBU selama masa konstruksi pabrik.

TKDN & 1% PPN Value-Added Tax

Syarat TKDN & Insentif PPN 1 Persen

Electric vehicles assembled in Indonesia with a Domestic Component Level (TKDN) of at least 40% receive a government VAT subsidy (PPN DTP), cutting the buyer's VAT from 11% down to just 1%, drastically lowering purchase prices.

Kendaraan listrik yang dirakit di Indonesia dengan Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri (TKDN) minimal 40% berhak memperoleh insentif PPN Ditanggung Pemerintah (PPN DTP), sehingga pembeli hanya membayar PPN 1% dari yang seharusnya 11%.

Nickel Downstreaming & HLI Karawang

Hilirisasi Nikel & Pabrik Sel Baterai HLI

Indonesia's nickel export ban fostered a complete supply chain from HPAL nickel processing in Morowali/Weda Bay to battery cell factories (PT HLI Green Power Karawang and Gotion High-Tech), creating a closed-loop regional EV powerhouse.

Kebijakan hilirisasi nikel mendorong ekosistem lengkap dari pengolahan HPAL di Morowali/Weda Bay hingga pabrik sel baterai di Karawang (PT HLI Green Power) dan Gotion High-Tech, menjadikan Indonesia pusat rantai pasok EV regional.
