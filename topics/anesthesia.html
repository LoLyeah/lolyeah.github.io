Anesthesia: Point-of-Care Clinical Suite & Resuscitation Engine

An uncompromising, offline-first point-of-care clinical decision suite engineered for anesthesiologists, intensivists, emergency physicians, and hospital code teams. Operating in electronically shielded surgical suites and sub-level ICUs with zero cloud latency, local IndexedDB persistence, calibrated 12-lead ECG simulation, and rapid-weight emergency push dosing. Sistem keputusan klinis point-of-care bebas latensi dan tanpa internet yang dirancang khusus untuk dokter spesialis anestesi, intensivis, dokter gawat darurat, dan tim resusitasi rumah sakit. Berfungsi di ruang operasi berpelindung dan ruang perawatan intensif dengan penyimpanan lokal IndexedDB, simulasi EKG 12-lead berkalibrasi, dan perhitungan dosis cepat berbasis berat badan.
