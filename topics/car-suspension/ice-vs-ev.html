ICE vs EV Chassis & Suspension Engineering Rekayasa Sasis & Suspensi ICE vs EV

Why electric vehicles demand higher spring rates, specialized hydraulic bushings, aggressive anti-squat geometry, and active damping. Mengapa kendaraan listrik memerlukan konstanta pegas lebih tinggi, bushing hidrolik khusus, geometri anti-squat agresif, dan peredaman aktif.

01. Battery Curb Weight & Skateboard Low Center of Gravity

EV battery packs add 300 to 700 kg of additional mass compared to equivalent ICE vehicles. However, because the battery is mounted underneath the cabin floor between the axles, the Center of Gravity (CG) is significantly lower. Baterai EV menambah massa 300 hingga 700 kg dibanding mobil ICE setara. Namun karena diletakkan di bawah lantai kabin di antara sumbu roda, Pusat Gravitasi (CG) jauh lebih rendah.

ICE Vehicles

High CG due to heavy engine block over front axle. Roll moment is higher, requiring stiffer anti-roll bars to prevent body lean.

EV Vehicles

Extremely low CG reduces roll moment naturally, but high overall mass creates massive kinetic energy under hard cornering and braking.

02. Instant Electric Torque & Anti-Squat Geometry

Electric motors deliver 100% of maximum torque instantly at 0 RPM. Without anti-squat suspension geometry, aggressive acceleration forces the rear suspension to compress severely, causing rear tire scrub and nose lift. Motor listrik memberikan 100% torsi maksimum secara instan pada 0 RPM. Tanpa geometri anti-squat, akselerasi agresif menekan suspensi belakang secara drastis.

Anti-Squat Angle

ICE cars typically use 15-30% anti-squat. EVs require 45-60%+ anti-squat geometry on control arm pivot axes to keep the body flat under launch.

Regenerative Load Transfer

One-pedal regenerative braking shifts load rapidly to the front axle, requiring high front anti-dive geometry.

03. Noise, Vibration, Harshness (NVH) without Engine Masking

In ICE cars, engine noise masks suspension thuds and road impact noise. EVs are completely silent, making mechanical suspension noise up to 10 dB more noticeable to cabin occupants. Pada mobil ICE, suara mesin menyamarkan benturan suspensi. EV sangat hening sehingga suara mekanis suspensi terasa hingga 10 dB lebih keras di kabin.

Hydraulic Bushings

EVs heavily utilize fluid-filled hydraulic control arm bushings to absorb high-frequency road vibrations without sacrificing steering response.

Acoustic Subframes

Subframes are mounted to the unibody chassis via dual-isolated rubber/silicone mounts to prevent road noise transmission.
