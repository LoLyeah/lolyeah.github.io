MODULE 01 // KINEMATICS & MASS DISTRIBUTION

Sprung Mass Inertia & Skateboard Low Center of Gravity

Lithium-ion battery packs add between 300 kg and 700 kg of extra curb weight to electric vehicles. However, because the battery pack is housed inside the floor structure between the front and rear axles, the Center of Gravity (CG) is significantly lower than in an ICE car with a high engine block. Baterai litium-ion menambah massa 300 kg hingga 700 kg pada kendaraan listrik. Namun karena baterai dipasang di bawah lantai di antara sumbu roda, Pusat Gravitasi (CG) jauh lebih rendah dibanding mobil ICE.

ICE Architecture (High CG)

Concentrated high mass in the engine bay over the front axle. Higher body roll moment requires stiffer mechanical anti-roll bars to prevent lean during cornering.

Skateboard EV Architecture (Low CG)

Very low CG reduces body roll naturally. The high sprung battery mass absorbs high-frequency road vibrations, yielding a floating ride quality over highway expansion joints.

PHYSICS FORMULA

Kinetic Energy Formula

\(E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2\)

Because kinetic energy scales linearly with mass \(m\), a 2.2-ton EV generates 35% higher kinetic energy under braking and suspension bump compression than a 1.6-ton ICE car at the same speed. Karena energi kinetik berbanding lurus dengan massa \(m\), EV seberat 2.2 ton menghasilkan energi kinetik 35% lebih besar saat pengereman.

MODULE 02 // LAUNCH KINEMATICS

Instant Electric Motor Torque & Anti-Squat Linkage Geometry

Electric motors produce 100% of maximum rated torque instantaneously at 0 RPM. When a high-performance EV launches, longitudinal acceleration forces create an extreme rotational moment that attempts to compress the rear suspension springs (rear squat) and lift the front nose. Motor listrik menghasilkan 100% torsi maksimum secara instan pada 0 RPM. Saat EV diluncurkan, gaya akselerasi menekan suspensi belakang (rear squat) dan mengangkat moncong depan.

ICE Anti-Squat Geometry (15% - 30%)

ICE engines build torque gradually along an RPM curve. Standard suspension link angles direct most launch force through springs and dampers.

EV Anti-Squat Geometry (45% - 60%+)

EV control arm pivot axes are inclined aggressively so that longitudinal motor torque is converted directly into mechanical link tension, locking the rear suspension flat without compressing springs.

MODULE 03 // SILENT CABIN NVH

NVH & Hydraulic Control Arm Bushings

In combustion cars, engine rumbling noise masks road surface impacts. EVs have silent electric powertrains, making suspension mechanical thuds up to 10 dB more audible to occupants. EVs utilize fluid-filled hydraulic bushings in lower control arms to absorb harsh impact frequencies while maintaining precise steering alignment. Pada mobil konvensional, suara mesin menyamarkan benturan jalan. EV sangat hening sehingga benturan mekanis suspensi terasa 10 dB lebih kencang. EV menggunakan bushing hidrolik berisi fluida untuk meredam getaran.

MODULE 04 // DURABILITY STANDARDS

High Load (HL) Tires & Bushing Wear Rates

The heavy curb mass of EVs combined with instant torque creates high shear stress on rubber bushings, ball joints, and tire treads. EVs require **High Load (HL)** index tires rated for higher inflation pressures (up to 44-50 psi) and reinforced control arm ball joints to prevent premature wear. Massa EV yang berat dikombinasikan dengan torsi instan menciptakan gesekan tinggi pada bushing karet dan ban. EV memerlukan ban bertanda **High Load (HL)** dan ball joint yang diperkuat.
