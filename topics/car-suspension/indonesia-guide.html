INDONESIAN ROAD TUNING & AFTERMARKET GUIDE

Optimizing Suspensions for Indonesian Road Conditions

Indonesian roads present unique suspension challenges: deep potholes, uneven expansion joints on elevated toll roads, heavy 7-passenger cargo loads, and tropical flood waters. Popular aftermarket shock absorber and spring upgrades in Indonesia include:

Tein EnduraPro (H.B.O. Tech)
Features Hydraulic Bump Stoppers (H.B.O.) to prevent violent bottoming out when hitting severe potholes at speed. Highly popular for Innova Zenix and Avanza.
Bilstein B6 / B8 Yellow Dampers
German mono-tube gas dampers providing firm body control, reducing high-speed float on Trans-Java toll roads for Fortuner and Pajero Sport.
Ironman 4x4 / Old Man Emu (OME)
Heavy-duty 2-inch lift springs and foam-cell shock absorbers built for Jimny, Hilux, and Fortuner off-road plantation routes.
