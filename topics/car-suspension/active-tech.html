MODULE 01 // 800V HIGH-VOLTAGE HYDRAULICS

Porsche Active Ride (PAR)

Porsche Active Ride revolutionizes suspension design by eliminating physical anti-roll bars entirely. Instead, each individual wheel damper is connected directly to a motor-pump unit operating on the vehicle's 400V/800V high-voltage battery network. The system dynamically pumps hydraulic fluid in and out of damper chambers up to 13 times per second (13 Hz), actively counteracting body roll during cornering and neutralizing pitch during heavy acceleration and braking. Porsche Active Ride merevolusi desain suspensi dengan mengeliminasi anti-roll bar fisik secara total. Setiap peredam roda terhubung langsung ke unit pompa motor yang beroperasi pada jaringan baterai 400V/800V. Sistem memompa fluida hidrolik hingga 13 kali per detik (13 Hz) untuk menetralkan gulingan dan pitch secara instan.

PORSCHE ACTIVE RIDE HIGHLIGHT

Zero Body Lean & Active Tilt

During cornering, PAR over-compensates body roll by actively tilting the car's body into the curve (like a motorcycle rider), reducing occupant lateral g-forces and maintaining zero pitch under 1.0g braking. Saat bermanuver, PAR memiringkan bodi mobil ke dalam tikungan (seperti pengendara motor), mengurangi gaya g lateral pada penumpang.

🚘 Models: Porsche Taycan (2024+), Panamera E-Hybrid
MODULE 02 // FULL-STACK NEV BODY CONTROL

BYD DiSus (Yun Nian) Full-Stack Ecosystem

Developed entirely in-house for New Energy Vehicles (NEVs), BYD's DiSus architecture provides a modular chassis control ecosystem categorized into four distinct technology tiers: Dikembangkan secara mandiri untuk Kendaraan Energi Baru (NEV), arsitektur DiSus BYD menyediakan ekosistem sasis modular yang terbagi dalam empat tingkatan:

DiSus-C (Intelligent Damping)
Continuous Damping Control solenoid valves adjusting damper oil flow in milliseconds for optimal ride comfort. (e.g. BYD Seal, Denza D9).
DiSus-A (Intelligent Air)
Multi-chamber air suspension adjusting both ground clearance (up to 150mm height travel) and spring stiffness dynamically.
DiSus-P (Intelligent Hydraulic)
Ultra-high pressure hydraulic active control featuring 200mm height travel, 3-wheel drive balance, and 1,000kg instant lift force (e.g. Yangwang U8).
DiSus-Z (Electromagnetic Linear)
Ultra-fast linear motor actuators replacing traditional hydraulic dampers, responding in 10 milliseconds with energy recovery capabilities (e.g. Yangwang U7).
MODULE 03 // CLOUD ROAD SCANNING

ClearMotion ClearRide & RoadMotion

ClearMotion replaces standard shock absorbers with high-bandwidth electro-hydraulic actuators paired with RoadMotion cloud surface mapping. By crowdsourcing road surface bump data from connected fleets, the vehicle previews incoming bumps down to the millimeter and pulls wheels up preemptively before impact. ClearMotion menggantikan peredam standar dengan aktuator elektro-hidrolik bandwidth tinggi yang terhubung ke cloud RoadMotion. Kendaraan memindai lubang jalan dan menarik roda ke atas sebelum menghantam benturan.

🚘 Production Deployment: Nio ET9 (SkyRide Chassis Platform)
MODULE 04 // ELECTROMAGNETIC & SOLENOID

MagneRide 4.0 & Solenoid CDC

MagneRide 4.0: Uses synthetic fluid infused with microscopic iron particles that instantly align when an electromagnetic coil is energized (adjusts 1,000 times/sec).
Continuous Damping Control (CDC): Uses external solenoid valves to adjust oil orifice size in real-time (Tenneco CVSAe / ZF CDC). MagneRide 4.0: Menggunakan fluida partikel besi yang sejajar secara instan saat koil dialiri listrik (menyesuaikan 1.000 kali/detik).
CDC: Katup solenoid eksternal mengatur ukuran lubang oli dalam milidetik.

🚘 Models: Cadillac Escalade V, Ferrari Purosangue, Chery Tiggo 8 Pro
MODULE 05 // REGENERATIVE CHASSIS TECH

Energy Harvesting & Regenerative Shock Absorbers

Traditional shock absorbers dissipate kinetic energy from road bumps as wasted heat into the atmosphere. Regenerative suspension systems (such as Audi e-rot and ClearMotion energy recovery) capture the up-and-down stroke fluid motion of the damper and run it through a small electric generator, converting suspension kinetic energy back into 48V/400V electricity to recharge the main battery pack. Peredam konvensional membuang energi kinetik dari benturan jalan sebagai panas. Sistem suspensi regeneratif (seperti Audi e-rot) menangkap gerakan naik-turun peredam dan mengalirkannya ke generator listrik kecil untuk mengisi kembali baterai kendaraan.
