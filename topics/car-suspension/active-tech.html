01. Porsche Active Ride (PAR)

Porsche Active Ride replaces mechanical anti-roll bars entirely. Each damper is connected directly to a motor-pump unit operating on the vehicle’s 400V/800V high-voltage EV network. The system dynamically pumps hydraulic fluid in and out of damper chambers up to 13 times per second (13 Hz), counteracting body roll and pitching forces before they manifest. Porsche Active Ride menggantikan anti-roll bar mekanis secara total. Setiap peredam terhubung langsung ke unit pompa motor yang beroperasi pada jaringan EV voltase tinggi 400V/800V. Sistem memompa fluida hidrolik hingga 13 kali per detik (13 Hz) untuk menetralkan gulingan dan pitch secara instan.

Power Architecture
400V / 800V High Voltage
Response Rate
13 Hz (13 adjustments / sec)
Key Innovation
Zero Anti-Roll Bar, Active Tilt

02. BYD DiSus (Yun Nian) Full-Stack Ecosystem

BYD's modular body control system covers 4 tiers: DiSus-C (Continuous Damping Control), DiSus-A (Air suspension height & stiffness), DiSus-P (Hydraulic active suspension with 4-wheel independent height/rigidity control capable of driving on 3 wheels), and DiSus-Z (Electromagnetic linear motors with millisecond response and energy recovery). Ekosistem kontrol sasis modular BYD mencakup 4 tingkat: DiSus-C (Peredam CDC), DiSus-A (Suspensi Udara), DiSus-P (Suspensi Hidrolik Aktif yang mampu berjalan 3 roda), dan DiSus-Z (Motor linier elektromagnetik kecepatan milidetik).

DiSus-P Capability
200mm Lift & 3-Wheel Balance
DiSus-Z Bandwidth
Millisecond Electromagnetic
Featured Models
BYD Seal, Denza D9, Yangwang U8/U9

03. ClearMotion ClearRide & RoadMotion

ClearMotion pairs high-bandwidth electro-hydraulic actuators with RoadMotion cloud software. By crowdsourcing road surface data from connected vehicles, the car previews incoming bumps and pulls the wheels up preemptively, isolating cabin occupants from chassis vibrations up to 5 Hz. ClearMotion menggabungkan aktuator elektro-hidrolik bandwidth tinggi dengan software cloud RoadMotion. Dengan mengumpulkan data permukaan jalan dari mobil terkoneksi, kendaraan menarik roda ke atas sebelum menghantam lubang.

Technology Focus
Predictive Cloud Surface Scan
Production Deployment
Nio ET9 (SkyRide Chassis)
