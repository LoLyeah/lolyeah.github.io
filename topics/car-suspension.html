MODULE 01 // FUNDAMENTALS

Suspension Physics & Geometry Fisika & Geometri Suspensi

Understanding how spring rates, damper curves, unsprung mass, and roll centers dictate vehicle dynamics. Memahami bagaimana konstanta pegas, kurva peredam, massa tak tersangga, dan pusat guling mengendalikan dinamika kendaraan.

1. Sprung vs Unsprung Mass Ratio

Sprung Mass: Everything supported by springs (body, engine/battery, occupants).
Unsprung Mass: Components directly touching/following the road (wheels, tires, brakes, hub assembly, lower control arms).
Rule: A high Sprung:Unsprung ratio yields smoother ride quality as body inertia dampens wheel movement. EVs benefit from higher sprung battery mass!

2. Anti-Squat & Anti-Dive Geometry

Angle of control arm pivot axes designed to convert longitudinal braking/acceleration forces into mechanical link forces rather than compression of springs.
EV Impact: EVs require up to 40% higher anti-squat due to instant electric motor torque causing extreme rear pitch.

3. Roll Center & Camber Gain

Roll Center: Theoretical point around which the vehicle body rolls during cornering.
Camber Gain: Increase in negative camber as the suspension compresses, keeping tire contact patch flat against the tarmac during heavy cornering.

MODULE 02 // ARCHITECTURE

Mechanical Suspension Architectures Arsitektur Suspensi Mekanis

The 6 core structural linkage layouts found in modern automobiles, complete with real-world car listings in Indonesia. 6 tata letak sambungan struktural utama pada mobil modern, dilengkapi daftar mobil nyata di pasar Indonesia.

📐
MECHANICAL (FRONT)

MacPherson Strut

Combines a telescopic shock absorber damper and coil spring into a single structural unit acting as the upper steering pivot. Simple, space-efficient, and ubiquitous in front-wheel drive cars. Menggabungkan peredam teleskopik dan pegas ulir menjadi satu unit struktural yang berfungsi sebagai poros kemudi atas. Ringkas, hemat ruang, dan sangat populer pada mobil FWD.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
🇮🇩 Toyota Innova Zenix 🇮🇩 Hyundai Ioniq 5 🇮🇩 Chery Omoda E5 🇮🇩 BYD Atto 3 🇮🇩 Mitsubishi Xpander Tesla Model 3 (Front)
✓ Compact & low cost
✗ Limited camber gain
🏁
MECHANICAL (PERFORMANCE)

Double Wishbone (A-Arm)

Uses two parallel A-shaped control arms to guide wheel motion. Offers precise camber control, excellent lateral stiffness, and superior handling stability under roll. Menggunakan dua arm berbentuk A yang sejajar untuk memandu gerak roda. Memberikan kontrol camber presisi, kekakuan lateral tinggi, dan stabilitas luar biasa saat bermanuver.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
🇮🇩 Toyota Fortuner (Front) 🇮🇩 Ford Everest 🇮🇩 Mazda CX-60 (Front) 🇮🇩 Lexus LX600 Porsche Taycan Tesla Model S
✓ Perfect camber control
✗ Consumes engine bay width
⛓️
MECHANICAL (INDEPENDENT)

Multi-Link (3/4/5-Link)

Uses 3 to 5 independent control arms per wheel to separately tune longitudinal compliance and lateral stiffness. Represents the gold standard for rear suspension balance. Menggunakan 3 hingga 5 lengan terpisah per roda untuk mengatur fleksibilitas longitudinal dan kekakuan lateral secara independen. Merupakan standar emas suspensi belakang.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
🇮🇩 Hyundai Ioniq 5 / 6 🇮🇩 BYD Seal 🇮🇩 Honda CR-V (6th Gen) 🇮🇩 BMW 330i Touring 🇮🇩 Toyota Innova Zenix (Rear)
✓ Ultimate ride & handling balance
✗ High component count & cost
🧱
SEMI-INDEPENDENT

Torsion Beam (Twist Beam)

A cross-beam connecting trailing arms that twists under uneven wheel deflection, acting as an integrated anti-roll bar. Maximizes boot space and battery floor width. Batang silang yang menghubungkan trailing arm dan melintir saat roda bergerak asimetris, berfungsi sekaligus sebagai anti-roll bar. Memaksimalkan ruang bagasi/baterai.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
🇮🇩 Wuling Air EV / Binguo EV 🇮🇩 Honda HR-V 🇮🇩 Toyota Yaris Cross 🇮🇩 Nissan Kicks e-POWER Peugeot 2008
✓ Low cost & max trunk space
✗ Wheel cross-coupling impact
🚛
DEPENDENT / HEAVY DUTY

Rigid Axle & Leaf / Coil Springs

Left and right wheels are rigidly connected by a solid axle housing. Offers rugged durability and extreme articulation for heavy payloads and off-road terrain. Roda kiri dan kanan terhubung kaku oleh selongsong gardan padat. Sangat tangguh dan berartikulasi tinggi untuk beban berat dan medan off-road ekstrim.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
🇮🇩 Suzuki Jimny (Rigid 3-Link) 🇮🇩 Toyota Hilux (Rear Leaf) 🇮🇩 Mitsubishi Pajero Sport (3-Link Coil) 🇮🇩 Isuzu D-Max
✓ Heavy payload & off-road durability
✗ High unsprung weight & bump steer
🏎️
INBOARD (MOTORSPORT)

Pushrod / Pullrod Inboard

Transfers wheel motion to internally mounted spring/damper units via rocker arms. Minimizes unsprung mass and improves aerodynamic airflow inside wheel wells. Mentransfer gerakan roda ke unit peredam/pegas yang terpasang di bagian dalam sasis via rocker arm. Meminimalkan massa tak tersangga dan meningkatkan aerodinamis.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
Formula 1 Racecars Lamborghini Revuelto Aston Martin Valkyrie Ford GT
✓ Lowest unsprung mass & aero clear
✗ Complex packaging & zero cargo
MODULE 03 // ELECTRONICS

Adaptive & Semi-Active Damping Peredam Adaptif & Semi-Aktif

How modern shock absorbers continuously alter fluid viscosity and valve orifice size in real-time. Bagaimana peredam kejut modern secara kontinu mengubah viskositas fluida dan ukuran katup dalam hitungan milidetik.

🧲
SEMI-ACTIVE

Magnetorheological (MagneRide)

Synthetic fluid infused with microscopic iron particles changes viscosity instantaneously when an electromagnetic coil inside the damper piston is energized (adjusts 1,000 times/sec). Fluida sintetis berisi partikel besi mikroskopis berubah viskositas secara instan saat koil elektromagnetik dialiri listrik (menyesuaikan 1.000 kali/detik).

🚘 VEHICLES
GM MagneRide 4.0 Cadillac Escalade V Ferrari Purosangue / SCM Chevy Corvette C8
⚡
SEMI-ACTIVE

Continuous Damping Control (CDC)

Uses high-speed electro-hydraulic solenoid valves outside or inside the damper to independently adjust rebound and compression oil flow in milliseconds. Menggunakan katup solenoid elektro-hidrolik berkecepatan tinggi untuk menyesuaikan aliran oli rebound dan kompresi secara terpisah dalam milidetik.

🚘 VEHICLES
🇮🇩 Chery Tiggo 8 Pro Max (CDC) 🇮🇩 BMW M Adaptive Suspension Tenneco CVSAe ZF CDC
🌊
PASSIVE FREQUENCY

Frequency Selective Damping (FSD)

A mechanical dual-valve damper that senses motion frequency. Softens damping on high-frequency rough roads for comfort while staying stiff on low-frequency body roll in corners. Peredam mekanis katup ganda yang mendeteksi frekuensi gerakan. Melembut pada jalan keriting (frekuensi tinggi) dan mengeras saat bermanuver (frekuensi rendah).

🚘 VEHICLES
Koni FSD Retrofits Alfa Romeo Tonale Jeep Compass
MODULE 04 // PNEUMATICS

Air & Pneumatic Springs Pegas Udara & Pneumatik

Replacing steel coil springs with pressurized air bladders to achieve self-leveling, dynamic height adjustment, and variable spring rates. Mengganti pegas baja dengan kantong udara bertekanan untuk mencapai penyesuaian tinggi otomatis, kontrol ketinggian dinamis, dan konstanta pegas variabel.

🎈
PNEUMATIC

Multi-Chamber Air Springs

Uses 2 or 3 distinct air chambers per spring unit. By opening or closing internal valves, the effective air volume changes instantly to switch between plush floating comfort and stiff sport handling. Menggunakan 2 atau 3 ruang udara terpisah per unit pegas. Dengan membuka/menutup katup internal, volume udara berubah seketika untuk beralih antara kenyamanan melayang atau performa spora yang kaku.

🚘 VEHICLE EXAMPLES
🇮🇩 Lexus LX600 (AHC Air) 🇮🇩 Porsche Cayenne 🇮🇩 Mercedes-Benz EQS / AIRMATIC Tesla Model X
MODULE 05 // THE CUTTING EDGE

Next-Gen Fully Active Suspensions (2024-2026+) Suspensi Aktif Generasi Terbaru (2024-2026+)

Systems that actively exert hydraulic or electromagnetic forces on each wheel to completely eliminate body roll, pitch, and dive. Sistem yang secara aktif memberikan gaya hidrolik atau elektromagnetik pada setiap roda untuk sepenuhnya menghilangkan gulingan, pitch, dan dive.

⚡
FULLY ACTIVE (400V/800V)

Porsche Active Ride (PAR)

Powered by the EV high-voltage battery. Each wheel damper is connected to a motor-pump unit that can pump hydraulic fluid in or out up to 13 times per second. Completely eliminates physical anti-roll bars; can lean into corners like a motorcycle and over-compensate pitch during heavy acceleration and braking. Ditenagai baterai voltase tinggi EV. Setiap peredam roda terhubung ke pompa motor hidrolik yang memompa fluida hingga 13 kali per detik. Sepenuhnya menghilangkan anti-roll bar fisik; dapat miring ke dalam tikungan seperti sepeda motor.

🚘 FEATURED MODELS
🇮🇩 Porsche Taycan (2024+) 🇮🇩 Porsche Panamera E-Hybrid
☁️
PROACTIVE CLOUD-CONNECTED

ClearMotion ClearRide

High-bandwidth electro-hydraulic actuators combined with "RoadMotion" cloud mapping technology. Scans and predicts upcoming road surface bumps down to the millimeter, pulling wheels up before impact. Aktuator elektro-hidrolik bandwidth tinggi dikombinasikan dengan pemetaan cloud "RoadMotion". Memindai dan memprediksi lubang jalan hingga ketelitian milimeter sebelum roda menghantamnya.

🚘 FEATURED MODELS
Nio ET9 / SkyRide Porsche Tech Partnership
🐲
FULL-STACK ECOSYSTEM

BYD DiSus (Yun Nian) Platform

BYD's modular body control architecture split into 4 tiers: DiSus-C (Damping), DiSus-A (Air), DiSus-P (Hydraulic - capability to drive on 3 wheels or jump), and DiSus-Z (Electromagnetic ultra-fast linear motors). Arsitektur sasis modular BYD yang terbagi 4 tingkatan: DiSus-C (Damping), DiSus-A (Air), DiSus-P (Hidrolik - mampu berjalan dengan 3 roda & melompat), dan DiSus-Z (Elektromagnetik linier super cepat).

MODULE 06 // ICE VS EV DEEP DIVE

ICE vs EV Suspension Engineering Differences Perbedaan Rekayasa Suspensi ICE vs EV

Why electric vehicles demand fundamentally different spring rates, anti-squat geometry, and bushing isolation compared to ICE vehicles. Mengapa kendaraan listrik memerlukan konstanta pegas, geometri anti-squat, dan insulasi bushing yang jauh berbeda dari kendaraan ICE.

Engineering Parameter ICE Vehicles (Engine & Tank) Electric Vehicles (Skateboard EV)
Mass Distribution & CG Concentrated high CG in engine block; variable fuel mass. Very low Center of Gravity (floor battery pack); 300–700kg extra curb weight.
Unsprung : Sprung Ratio Lower sprung mass means unsprung wheel impacts are felt more easily. Extremely heavy sprung mass dampens road impacts, but heavy wheels stress bushings.
Instant Torque Squat Gradual engine torque build-up. Standard anti-squat angles (15-30%). Instant 0-RPM torque. Requires aggressive anti-squat geometry (>45%) to prevent rear squat.
NVH & Sound Masking Engine noise masks tire and suspension mechanical thuds. Silent powertrain makes suspension thuds and road noise glaringly obvious; requires hydraulic bushings.
Active Power Source Limited to 12V / 48V mild-hybrid electrical architecture. Direct access to 400V/800V high-voltage traction battery for high-draw hydraulic pumps.
