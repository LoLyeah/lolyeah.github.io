Mathematical Formulations of Ecological Boundaries

1. Walter-Lieth Aridity Criterion
P < 2 × T

When monthly precipitation (in mm) drops below twice the mean monthly temperature (in °C), potential evapotranspiration outstrips rainfall, entering water-stress aridity.

2. Holdridge PET Ratio
PET = (58.93 × T_bio) / MAP

Holdridge's Potential Evapotranspiration Ratio. Values <1.0 indicate humid moisture surplus; values >1.0 indicate semi-arid to arid deficits.

3. Bailey's Moisture Index
M = MAP / (MAT + 10)

Evaluates climatic continentality and drought severity index across temperate steppe and woodland transitions.
