Epipelagic Zone (0–200m)

The euphotic sunlit layer where photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) supports phytoplankton, kelp, and hermatypic coral reefs. Over 90% of all marine life operates within or visits this zone for sustenance.

ECOSYSTEM SPECIALIZATION • 6 KEY DOMAINS

The 6 Critical Aquatic & Oceanic Ecosystems

🪸 Depth: 0–30m

Coral Reef Ecosystems

Rainforests of the sea built on CaCO₃ aragonite frameworks via endosymbiosis with Zooxanthellae algae. Vulnerable to heatwave bleaching.

NPP: ~2,500 g C/m²/yr (Highest Marine)
Threat: Ocean acidification & bleaching
🦀 Coastal Intertidal

Mangroves & Blue Carbon

Halophytic coastal trees adapted to tidal saltwater. Sequester carbon up to 10x faster per hectare into anoxic sediments than terrestrial rainforests.

Carbon Burial: ~1,000 t C/ha locked in mud
Key Feature: Ultrafiltration root membranes
🦦 Temperate Coastal 2–30m

Giant Kelp Forests

Dominated by giant brown algae growing up to 45 cm/day in cold upwelling currents. Maintained by sea otters preventing urchin barren collapse.

Growth Rate: 30–45 cm per day
Trophic Cascade: Otter → Urchin → Kelp
🐋 Open Ocean Epipelagic

Pelagic Photic Ocean

Vast open waters driven by microscopic cyanobacteria (Prochlorococcus). Accounts for over half of planetary photosynthetic oxygen generation.

Oxygen Output: ~50% of planetary atmosphere
Limiting Nutrient: Iron (Fe) & Nitrogen (N)
🌋 Abyssal 2,000–4,000m

Hydrothermal Vents & Chemosynthesis

Ecosystems independent of sunlight. Chemosynthetic bacteria oxidize H₂S mineral fluids to sustain giant gutless Riftia tubeworms and vent crabs.

Energy Source: H₂S + O₂ → Sulfate + Energy
Key Organism: Riftia pachyptila (Trophosome)
🛶 Lentic Freshwater

Lakes & Thermal Overturn

Temperate lakes stratify into Epilimnion and Hypolimnion. Water reaches maximum density at 3.98°C, triggering spring and autumn turnover cycles.

Peak Density: Exactly 3.98°C
Stratification: Epilimnion • Thermocline • Hypolimnion
