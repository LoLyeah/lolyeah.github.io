Sementara Mild Hybrid (MHEV) hanya menghemat ~10% BBM karena kompresor AC tetap membebani mesin saat macet tropis. In the comparable ~Rp 390M–520M crossover & MPV class, Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs like BYD M6) deliver the lowest TCO from day one due to MSRP parity with ICE (Rp 429M), ~75% cheaper home-charging fuel cost (Rp 257/km vs Rp 1,087/km petrol), and 0% annual PKB vehicle tax (Permendagri 6/2023). Full Hybrids (HEVs like Yaris Cross) recover their Rp 10M price premium over ICE in just 15,600 km (~9 months) and retain an Rp 88M cumulative advantage at 200,000 km even after an out-of-warranty Rp 30M battery swap. Mild Hybrids (MHEVs) save only ~10% fuel as heavy tropical AC load forces constant engine idling.

Formulasi Matematis Mathematical Modeling

Metodologi & Rumus Biaya Kumulatif Methodology & TCO Formulation

Total Cost of Ownership pada jarak $d$ (km) dimodelkan secara piecewise continuous: Total Cost of Ownership at distance $d$ (km) is modeled as a piecewise continuous trajectory:

FUNGSI BIAYA KUMULATIF TCO: CUMULATIVE TCO COST FUNCTION: C(d) = P_0 + (r_{energi} + r_{servis} + r_{ban}) \times d + T_{PKB}(d) + M(d)
• P_0 = Harga Beli OTR Jakarta (MSRP) pada km 0.Acquisition MSRP at km 0.
• r_{energi} = Biaya energi per km = $(Harga\,BBM / FE)$ untuk ICE/HEV, atau $(kWh/100km \times Tarif\,PLN / 100) \times 1.15$ (losses AC-DC) untuk BEV.Energy expenditure per km = $(Fuel\,Price / FE)$ for ICE/HEV, or $(kWh/100km \times PLN\,Rate / 100) \times 1.15$ (AC-DC charging loss) for BEV.
• r_{servis} = Servis berkala per km (oli mesin, filter, busi, transmisi, radiator coolant).Routine servicing per km (engine oil, filters, spark plugs, fluids).
• r_{ban} = Keausan ban per km. BEV memakai ban khusus High-Load (HL) dengan keausan ~25% lebih cepat akibat bobot baterai dan torsi instan.Tire wear per km. BEVs require High-Load (HL) spec tires wearing out ~25% faster due to battery curb weight and instant torque.
• T_{PKB}(d) = Pajak Kendaraan Bermotor kumulatif: $PKB_{tahunan} \times (d / 20.000)$. BEV dikenai tarif 0% sesuai Permendagri No. 6/2023.Cumulative annual vehicle tax: $PKB_{annual} \times (d / 20,000)$. BEVs enjoy 0% PKB rate under Permendagri No. 6/2023.
• M(d) = Step-jump milestone penggantian baterai pada 120.000 km (+Rp 30 Juta untuk HEV, +Rp 15 Juta MHEV 48V).Battery replacement step-jump milestone at 120,000 km (+Rp 30M for HEV, +Rp 15M for 48V MHEV).
Rumus Titik Impas (Breakeven Distance): Breakeven Distance Formulation:
d_{crossover} = \frac{\Delta P_0 + \Delta M}{\Delta r_{running}}
Titik di mana penghematan biaya per kilometer ($\Delta r$) melunasi selisih harga awal kendaraan ($\Delta P_0$) dan milestone baterai ($\Delta M$). The exact mileage where running cost savings per km ($\Delta r$) amortize initial MSRP price premium ($\Delta P_0$) and battery replacement capital ($\Delta M$).
Regulasi & Kebijakan Resmi RI Indonesian Tax & Regulatory Policies

Dasar Hukum & Insentif Perpajakan RI Legal Framework & Tax Policies

Permendagri No. 6/2023 & UU HKPD No. 1/2022

Pajak Kendaraan Bermotor (PKB) dan Bea Balik Nama (BBNKB) untuk Kendaraan Bermotor Listrik Berbasis Baterai (BEV) ditetapkan sebesar 0%. Pemilik mobil listrik hanya membayar SWDKLLJ Jasa Raharja sebesar Rp 143.000 / tahun. Annual Motor Vehicle Tax (PKB) and Vehicle Title Transfer Fee (BBNKB) for pure Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) are enacted at 0%. EV owners only pay mandatory SWDKLLJ insurance of IDR 143,000 / year.

PP No. 74/2021 (PPnBM Karbon Emisi)

Pajak Penjualan atas Barang Mewah (PPnBM) mobil hybrid (HEV) berkisar 6% – 8% untuk emisi CO2 <100 g/km atau konsumsi BBM >23 km/L, sedangkan mobil bensin konvensional (ICE) dikenakan 15% – 40%. Carbon-tax luxury sales tax (PPnBM) for Full Hybrids (HEVs) is preferential at 6% – 8% for low-emission vehicles, whereas standard gasoline ICE models are levied between 15% and 40%.

Pergub DKI Jakarta No. 88/2019 (Ganjil-Genap)

Mobil listrik murni (BEV) plat biru mendapatkan pembebasan 100% dari aturan ganjil-genap koridor protokol Jakarta. Mobil Mild Hybrid (MHEV), Full Hybrid (HEV), dan Series Hybrid (EREV) tetap terkena aturan ganjil genap. Pure electric vehicles (BEVs) with official blue-striped license plates are 100% exempt from Jakarta's Odd-Even peak traffic restrictions. Mild (MHEV), Full (HEV), and Series (EREV) hybrids remain restricted.

Garansi Baterai APM Resmi Indonesia (8 Tahun / 160.000 km)

Mayoritas APM (Toyota, Honda, Hyundai, BYD, Wuling) memberikan garansi resmi baterai traksi hingga 8 tahun atau 160.000 km. Milestone lonjakan pada grafik (120.000–160.000 km) mengasumsikan biaya penggantian sel mandiri pasca-garansi atau keausan iklim tropis ekstrem. Most Indonesian distributors provide 8-year or 160,000 km traction battery factory warranties. The 120,000 km milestone step-jump conservatively factors in post-warranty cell module refurbishment or heavy tropical ambient degradation.
