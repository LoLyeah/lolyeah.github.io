Subpage 02: Chassis & Suspension Engineering Guide Subhalaman 02: Panduan Rekayasa Sasis & Suspensi

An in-depth technical analysis comparing suspension architectures across all 26 GIIAS 2026 booth entry cars: MacPherson Strut, Torsion Beam, Multi-Link Independent, Double Wishbone, Heavy-Duty Solid Axles Dana 44, and EV battery mass damping dynamics. Analisis teknis mendalam membandingkan arsitektur suspensi di 26 mobil entry booth GIIAS 2026: MacPherson Strut, Torsion Beam, Multi-Link Independen, Double Wishbone, Gardan Solid Dana 44, serta dinamika peredaman bobot baterai EV.

1. MacPherson Strut + Torsion Beam

The most cost-effective and compact suspension architecture. Offers simple maintenance and small packaging, ideal for budget city hatchbacks and MPVs, but exhibits wheel camber changes over severe bumps. Arsitektur suspensi paling ekonomis & ringkas. Mudah dirawat dan tidak memakan ruang kabin, cocok untuk hatchback perkotaan & MPV budget, namun mengalami perubahan camber saat melintasi lubang dalam.

Featured on: Honda Brio, Suzuki S-Presso, Daihatsu Ayla, BYD Atto 1, Mitsubishi Xpander, Nissan Serena, Kia Sonet Digunakan pada: Honda Brio, Suzuki S-Presso, Daihatsu Ayla, BYD Atto 1, Mitsubishi Xpander, Nissan Serena, Kia Sonet

2. Multi-Link Independent Rear Suspension

Features 4 to 5 independent control arms per wheel to maintain optimal tire contact patch and road grip. Essential for handling high EV curb weights and providing smooth luxury ride quality. Memiliki 4 hingga 5 lengan kontrol independen per roda untuk menjaga kontak tapak ban & traksi maksimal. Sangat penting menangani bobot baterai EV yang berat & memberikan kenyamanan kelas atas.

Featured on: Geely EX2 Pro, Jaecoo J5 EV, Leapmotor C10, BMW 218i Gran Coupe, Mercedes-Benz GLB 200, Audi S3 Digunakan pada: Geely EX2 Pro, Jaecoo J5 EV, Leapmotor C10, BMW 218i Gran Coupe, Mercedes-Benz GLB 200, Audi S3

3. Front Double Wishbone Suspension

Utilizes upper and lower A-arms to keep wheels perpendicular to the road surface during extreme cornering and off-road articulation. Delivers supreme high-speed stability and precision. Menggunakan arm A atas & bawah untuk menjaga posisi roda tetap tegak lurus terhadap permukaan jalan saat menikung tajam & artikulasi off-road. Memberikan stabilitas kecepatan tinggi terbaik.

Featured on: XPeng G6 Pro, Subaru Crosstrek (Rear), Ford Ranger Wildtrak, Volvo XC60 Digunakan pada: XPeng G6 Pro, Subaru Crosstrek (Belakang), Ford Ranger Wildtrak, Volvo XC60

4. Heavy-Duty Solid Axles (Dana 44)

Solid beam axle architecture engineered for extreme off-road rock crawling and 4x4 articulation. Features electronic sway bar disconnect to allow maximum wheel droop over boulders. Arsitektur gardan solid tugas berat yang dirancang khusus melibas bebatuan off-road ekstrem. Dilengkapi pelepas sway bar elektronik untuk artikulasi roda maksimal.

Featured on: Jeep Wrangler Rubicon (Heavy-Duty Dana 44 Solid Front & Rear Axles) Digunakan pada: Jeep Wrangler Rubicon (Gardan Solid Dana 44 Depan & Belakang)
