The core engine of modern foundation models. Vaswani et al. (2017) discarded recurrence in favor of permutation-equivariant pairwise token routing modulated by learned query, key, and value matrices.

State Space Models (Mamba) & Sparse Mixture of Experts

Continuous LTI to Selective SSM (Mamba)

Continuous state-space equations map 1D sequence $x(t)$ to hidden state $h(t)$ via differential equations:

h'(t) = A h(t) + B x(t)
y(t) = C h(t)

Albert Gu and Tri Dao (2023) introduced Selective State Spaces by making $B, C,$ and step size $\Delta$ input-dependent functions of token $x_t$. This breaks linear time-invariance, allowing the model to selectively remember relevant tokens and forget noise with linear $O(T)$ inference memory.

Sparse Mixture of Experts (MoE)

Rather than activating all feedforward parameters per token, MoE replaces the dense FFN block with $E$ parallel sub-networks, routed by a softmax gating network:

y = \sum_{i \in \text{TopK}} \text{Softmax}(x W_g)_i \cdot \text{FFN}_i(x)

In DeepSeek-V3, 671 billion total parameters are distributed across 256 fine-grained routed experts plus 1 shared expert. Each token activates only 37 billion active parameters, achieving frontier reasoning capabilities at a fraction of dense inference FLOPs.
