Industrial Anchors

Key Battery Vendors & Ecosystem Players in Indonesia

HLI Green Power

Cell Plant

Joint venture between Hyundai Motor Group and LG Energy Solution in Karawang, West Java. 10 GWh NCMA battery cell facility supplying Hyundai Ioniq 5, Kona Electric, and regional EV assembly plants.

Location:Karawang, West Java
Capacity:10 GWh / year
Partners:Hyundai + LG Energy Solution

CBL / CATL Consortium

$5.9B Project

Grand package joint venture between CATL (Brunp/Lygend), PT Antam, and IBC. Covers end-to-end nickel mining, HPAL refining, precursor/cathode, and 6.9 to 15 GWh battery cell manufacturing facility.

Location:Karawang & North Maluku
Target Cap:15 GWh Cell Plant
Partners:CATL, Antam, IBC

IBC (Indonesia Battery Corp)

State Entity

State-owned consortium formed by PLN, Pertamina, PT Antam, and MIND ID. Spearheads national EV battery cell standards, battery swapping ecosystem for 2-wheelers (Gesits, Alva, Electrum), and BESS storage.

Scope:2W/4W Cells & Swapping
Shareholders:PLN, Pertamina, Antam, MIND ID

PT Vale Indonesia / Ford / Huayou

HPAL Refinery

Pomalaa HPAL facility in Southeast Sulawesi producing Mixed Hydroxide Precipitate (MHP). Strategic collaboration between PT Vale, Zhejiang Huayou Cobalt, and Ford Motor Company.

Location:Pomalaa, SE Sulawesi
Output:120,000 Tons MHP/year
Partners:Vale, Huayou, Ford

Harita Nickel (PT TBP)

HPAL Pioneer

First commercial HPAL operator in Indonesia on Obi Island, North Maluku. Converts limonite nickel ore into MHP, Nickel Sulfate (NiSO4), and Cobalt Sulfate (CoSO4) for EV precursor manufacturing.

Location:Obi Island, North Maluku
Products:MHP, NiSO4, CoSO4

Gotion High-Tech / Wuling

Pack Assembly

Supplies localized LFP battery pack assemblies for PT SGMW (Wuling Motors Indonesia) powering Air EV, Binguo EV, and Cloud EV models to meet local TKDN requirements.

Scope:LFP Pack Integration
Key Client:Wuling Motors Indonesia

TKDN Local Content Regulations

  • 40% TKDN requirement for 4W Electric Vehicles through 2025.
  • Escalates to 60% TKDN requirement once local battery cells (HLI/CATL) are integrated.
  • Qualifying EVs receive 1% Luxury Goods Sales Tax (PPnBM) & 1% VAT subsidy.
  • Mandates localized battery pack assembly and cell sourcing by 2026.

ESG & EU Battery Passport Transition

  • Decarbonizing HPAL refining: Transitioning captive coal power plants to hydro & solar energy.
  • Compliance with European Union (EU) Battery Regulation carbon footprint declarations.
  • Tailings management: Dry stacking technology replaces Deep Sea Tailings Placement (DSTP).
  • IRMA (Initiative for Responsible Mining Assurance) audits for nickel concessions.
