1. Market Landscape & Trends

1. Format A: Indonesian EV Battery Market Landscape & Trends

1.1 Downstreaming (Hilirisasi) & Policy Catalysts

Indonesia's EV battery landscape is dictated by national downstreaming laws and Presidential Regulation No. 79/2023. The Ministry of Industry mandates a 40% Domestic Content (TKDN) requirement rising to 60% by 2027. Furthermore, import incentives for Completely Built-Up (CBU) electric vehicles expire in 2026, forcing OEM automakers (BYD, VinFast, Chery, Wuling) to establish local assembly and contract local battery cell/pack suppliers.

1.2 Industrial Battery Clusters in Java & Maluku

Cell manufacturing and pack assembly are concentrated in the Bekasi–Cikarang–Karawang industrial belt in West Java (housing HLI Green Power, CATL PT CATIB, and Gotion Indonesia), while upstream HPAL nickel refining and intermediate materials (MHP/matte) are centered in Morowali (Central Sulawesi) and Weda Bay (North Maluku).

1.3 Key Industry Trends

  • Shift from Cell Import to In-Country Cell Gigafactories: HLI Green Power is actively producing local cells for Hyundai, while CATL (PT CATIB) is accelerating its 6.9 GWh Karawang cell factory for Q3 2026 launch.
  • LFP Chemistry Dominance: Over 85% of 4W passenger EVs (Wuling, BYD, Chery, MG, Neta) and 2W scooters in Indonesia utilize LFP due to cost competitiveness and thermal safety.
  • 2W Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) Standardization: Battery swapping operators (Swap Energy, IBC, Voltron) dominate 2W fleet adoption (Gojek/Grab), removing upfront battery costs for price-sensitive drivers.

Key Takeaway Explainer

  • Hyundai vs. Wuling/BYD Localization Strategy: Hyundai leads 4W cell localization in Indonesia via its HLI Green Power joint venture in Karawang, powering the All-New Kona Electric and Ioniq 5. Meanwhile, Wuling localizes pack assembly through Gotion Indonesia in Cikarang for the Air EV and Binguo EV. BYD currently imports its FinDreams Blade battery packs but plans local vehicle assembly in Subang by 2026.
  • The 2W Domestic Battery Ecosystem: ABC Lithium (PT Intercallin) is Indonesia's pioneer domestic cell manufacturer, producing 18650 LFP cells used by Swap Energy for Smoot e-scooters. State-owned IBC partners with local motorcycle leaders (Gesits, Alva, Polytron, Volta) to establish standardized 2W battery dimensions.

4. Conclusion

4. Conclusion & Strategic Outlook

Summary & Final Outlook

Indonesia's EV battery landscape is transitioning rapidly from import-reliant pack assembly to localized cell manufacturing. Automakers in the 4W passenger EV segment (Hyundai, Wuling, BYD, Chery, MG) must secure local cell supply from HLI Green Power and CATL (PT CATIB) to meet 60% TKDN rules by 2027. In the 2W segment, local cell pioneer ABC Lithium and state-backed IBC along with swapping networks (Swap Energy, Voltron) are standardizing battery infrastructure to drive mass adoption.
