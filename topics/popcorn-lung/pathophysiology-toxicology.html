Molecular Pathophysiology & Toxicological Cascade of Constrictive Bronchiolitis Patofisiologi Molekuler & Kaskade Toksikologis Bronkiolitis Konstriktif

Inhalation of volatile alpha-diketones—principally diacetyl (2,3-butanedione) and 2,3-pentanedione—triggers high-affinity protein dicarbonyl adduct cross-linking, epithelial necrosis of club cells in terminal bronchioles (<2 mm), sustained TGF-β1 fibroproliferation, and irreversible concentric luminal scarring. Inhalasi senyawa volatil alfa-diketon—terutama diasetil (2,3-butanadion) dan 2,3-pentanadion—memicu pembentukan ikatan silang aduk dikarbonil protein berafinitas tinggi, nekrosis sel epitel club pada bronkiolus terminal (<2 mm), fibroproliferasi TGF-β1 berkelanjutan, dan parut konsentris luminal yang ireversibel.

2,3-Butanedione CAS 431-03-8

Diacetyl

A naturally occurring low-molecular-weight vicinal diketone with an intense buttery aroma. Boils at 88°C with high vapor pressure (56.8 mmHg at 25°C). Highly volatile when heated in flavor mixing tanks, coffee roasting ovens, or electronic cigarette atomizers. Diketone visinal dengan berat molekul rendah yang terbentuk secara alami dengan aroma mentega intens. Titik didih 88°C dengan tekanan uap tinggi (56,8 mmHg pada 25°C). Sangat volatil saat dipanaskan di tangki pencampuran perisa atau atomizer rokok elektrik.

Formula: CH3-CO-CO-CH3
NIOSH REL: 5 ppb (8-hr TWA)
Acetylpropionyl CAS 600-14-6

2,3-Pentanedione

Introduced by manufacturers as a "diacetyl substitute" following the 2002 Missouri factory revelations. Animal toxicological trials (Morgan et al., 2012) demonstrated equal or greater epithelial necrosis and neurotoxic apoptosis, debunking regrettable substitution. Diperkenalkan oleh produsen sebagai "pengganti diasetil" pasca kasus pabrik Missouri 2002. Uji toksikologi hewan membuktikan nekrosis epitel dan apoptosis sel saraf yang setara atau bahkan lebih berat dibanding diasetil.

Formula: CH3-CO-CO-CH2-CH3
NIOSH REL: 9.3 ppb (8-hr TWA)
3-Hydroxybutanone CAS 513-86-0

Acetoin

A monoketone hydroxy intermediate. Possesses significantly reduced chemical reactivity compared to adjacent dicarbonyls because the hydroxyl group cannot form dual Schiff base adducts. However, high-temperature aerosolization can catalyze oxidation back into diacetyl. Intermediet hidroksi monoketon. Memiliki reaktivitas kimia jauh lebih rendah dibanding dikarbonil karena gugus hidroksil tidak membentuk aduk basa Schiff ganda. Namun aerosol suhu tinggi dapat mengatalisis oksidasi kembali menjadi diasetil.

Formula: CH3-CO-CH(OH)-CH3
Risk Profile: Secondary Oxidative Precursor

01

Covalent Adduct Formation & Clara / Club Cell Necrosis Pembentukan Aduk Kovalen & Nekrosis Sel Club

Inhaled diacetyl readily penetrates the distal respiratory bronchioles, where airflow slows to near-diffusion velocities. The electrophilic 1,2-dicarbonyl moiety forms covalent bonds with nucleophilic guanidino groups on arginine residues and amino groups on lysine, triggering severe dicarbonyl stress. Secretory club cells (formerly Clara cells), which produce protective surfactant protein SCGB1A1, suffer immediate mitochondrial uncoupling, loss of tight-junction integrity (claudin-1, occludin), and necrotic detachment into the bronchiolar lumen. Diasetil terinhalasi dengan cepat menembus bronkiolus respiratorius distal tempat aliran udara melambat. Gugus elektrofilik 1,2-dikarbonil membentuk ikatan kovalen dengan residu arginin dan lisin, memicu stres dikarbonil berat. Sel club sekretori yang menghasilkan protein pelindung SCGB1A1 mengalami disfungsi mitokondria mendadak, kehilangan integritas tight-junction (claudin-1, occludin), dan nekrosis eksfoliatif ke dalam lumen bronkiolus.

Key Biomarkers: ↓ SCGB1A1, ↑ Lactate Dehydrogenase (LDH), Epithelial Denudation
02

Neutrophilic Infiltration & Pro-Inflammatory Cytokine Storm Infiltrasi Neutrofil & Pelepasan Sitokin Pro-Inflamasi

Denudation of the bronchiolar basement membrane exposes the submucosa to direct airborne chemical insult. Activated resident alveolar macrophages and surviving epithelial cells release copious chemokines: interleukin-8 (CXCL8), interleukin-1β (IL-1β), and tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α). Neutrophils rapidly extravasate into the peribronchiolar interstitium, discharging myeloperoxidase (MPO) and matrix metalloproteinases (MMP-2 and MMP-9) that degrade extracellular elastin and collagen scaffoldings. Denudasi membran basalis mengekspos submukosa ke paparan kimia langsung. Makrofag alveolar teraktivasi dan sel epitel tersisa melepaskan kemokin dalam jumlah masif: interleukin-8 (CXCL8), IL-1β, dan TNF-α. Neutrofil segera bermigrasi ke interstisium peribronkiolar, melepaskan mieloperoksidase (MPO) dan matriks metaloproteinase (MMP-2, MMP-9) yang merusak kerangka elastin dan kolagen.

Key Mediators: ↑↑ CXCL8 (IL-8), ↑ MMP-9, ↑ MPO, Neutrophil Extravasation
03

Aberrant Myofibroblastic Proliferation & TGF-β1 Upregulation Proliferasi Miofibroblas & Regulasi Naik TGF-β1

Instead of normal epithelial restitution, persistent injury drives excessive transforming growth factor-beta 1 (TGF-β1) and platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF) signaling. Submucosal mesenchymal fibroblasts transition into α-smooth muscle actin (α-SMA)-positive myofibroblasts. These contractile cells synthesize disordered, dense Type I and Type III collagen bundles, accumulating inside the bronchiolar submucosa and circumferential peribronchiolar space. Alih-alih terjadi epitelisasi normal, cedera berkelanjutan memicu lonjakan sinyal transforming growth factor-beta 1 (TGF-β1) dan PDGF. Fibroblas mesenkim berdiferensiasi menjadi miofibroblas positif α-SMA yang kontraktil. Sel-sel ini menyintesis bundel kolagen tipe I dan III padat yang menumpuk di submukosa dan ruang sirkumferensial peribronkiolar.

Pathologic Transition: Fibroblast → Myofibroblast (α-SMA+), ↑↑ TGF-β1
04

Concentric Cicatricial Stenosis & Total Airway Obliteration Stenosis Sikatriks Konsentris & Obliterasi Total Saluran Napas

The mature collagen contracture forms a concentric collar around the membranous bronchiole. The airway internal lumen pinches shut from the outside inward (constrictive bronchiolitis). Unlike Cryptogenic Organizing Pneumonia (COP/BOOP), where intraluminal polypoid granulation tissue plugs can be dissolved with corticosteroids, this concentric cicatrix represents irreversible mature fibrotic tissue. Distal alveoli remain intact but mechanically isolated, producing fixed severe air trapping. Kontraktur kolagen matang membentuk kerah konsentris di sekeliling bronkiolus membranosa. Lumen internal terjepit menutup dari luar ke dalam (bronkiolitis konstriktif). Berbeda dari COP/BOOP yang memiliki sumbatan polipoid intraluminal yang responsif terhadap steroid, sikatriks konsentris ini adalah jaringan fibrotik matang yang ireversibel. Alveoli distal tetap utuh namun terisolasi secara mekanis, menghasilkan air trapping berat yang permanen.

End State: Concentric Luminal Scarring, Complete Non-Reversibility
