Clinical Diagnostic Standards for Constrictive Bronchiolitis Standar Diagnostik Klinis Bronkiolitis Konstriktif

Popcorn lung frequently masquerades as refractory adult-onset asthma or atypical COPD. Establishing a definitive diagnosis requires demonstrating the classic physiologic triad: fixed non-reversible airflow obstruction, severe plethysmographic air trapping with preserved DLCO, and expiratory HRCT mosaic attenuation. Popcorn lung sering kali tersamar sebagai asma refrakter dewasa atau PPOK atipikal. Penegakan diagnosis definitif menuntut pembuktian trias fisiologis klasik: obstruksi jalan napas menetap tanpa reversibilitas bronkodilator, air trapping pletismografi dengan DLCO yang dipertahankan, dan mosaic attenuation pada HRCT ekspirasi.

The Pulmonary Function Test (PFT) Diagnostic Triad Trias Diagnostik Uji Fungsi Paru (PFT)

Three essential physiological hallmarks that separate constrictive bronchiolitis from asthma, COPD, and pulmonary fibrosis Tiga penanda fisiologis esensial yang membedakan bronkiolitis konstriktif dari asma, PPOK, dan fibrosis paru

01

Fixed Airflow Obstruction (Post-BD Non-Reversible) Obstruksi Jalan Napas Menetap (Non-Reversibel Pasca-BD)

FEV1/FVC ratio is diminished (<0.70 or below the lower limit of normal). When administered 400 μg inhaled albuterol/salbutamol, FEV1 demonstrates zero or minimal response (<12% and <200 mL increase). This proves the stenosis is structural cicatricial fibrosis rather than smooth muscle bronchospasm. Rasio FEV1/FVC menurun (<0,70 atau di bawah batas bawah normal). Setelah pemberian 400 μg albuterol/salbutamol, FEV1 tidak menunjukkan perbaikan signifikan (<12% dan <200 mL). Ini membuktikan stenosis bersifat fibrosis struktural permanen, bukan spasme otot polos.

Criterion: ΔFEV1 < 200 mL & < 12%
02

Marked Hyperinflation & Severe Air Trapping Hiperinflasi Nyata & Air Trapping Berat

Body plethysmography reveals dramatic elevation of Residual Volume (RV > 120% predicted) and RV/TLC ratio (>35-40%). Functional Residual Capacity (FRC) is also elevated. Expiratory premature airway closure traps liters of air behind stenosed terminal bronchioles that cannot escape during forceful exhalation. Pletismografi tubuh mendeteksi lonjakan dramatis Volume Residu (RV > 120% prediksi) dan rasio RV/TLC (>35-40%). FRC juga meningkat. Penutupan dini bronkiolus yang menyempit menjebak udara dalam jumlah besar di alveoli distal saat ekspirasi paksa.

Criterion: RV > 120% pred · RV/TLC > 35%
03

Preserved Carbon Monoxide Diffusion (DLCO) Kapasitas Difusi Karbon Monoksida (DLCO) Utuh

Single-breath diffusing capacity for carbon monoxide (DLCO) remains within normal or near-normal limits (>75-80% predicted). Because the toxic insult targets terminal conducting bronchioles rather than the alveolar-capillary gas-exchange membrane, alveolar surface area and hemoglobin transfer remain physiologically intact. Kapasitas difusi gas karbon monoksida (DLCO) tetap dalam batas normal atau mendekati normal (>75-80% prediksi). Karena cedera toksik terfokus pada saluran penghantar bronkiolus terminal dan bukan pada membran kapiler-alveolar, luas permukaan pertukaran gas tetap utuh.

Criterion: DLCO ≥ 75% predicted (normal)

Paired Inspiratory vs Expiratory HRCT Protocol Protokol HRCT Berpasangan Inspirasi vs Ekspirasi

Why standard inspiratory CT scans frequently miss constrictive bronchiolitis, and how dynamic expiratory thin-slice scanning exposes occult mosaic attenuation Mengapa CT inspirasi standar kerap luput mendeteksi bronkiolitis konstriktif, dan bagaimana pemindaian irisan tipis ekspirasi dinamis mengungkap mosaic attenuation

Full Inspiration Phase -700 to -800 HU

Inspiratory HRCT: Often Normal or Deceptively Subtle HRCT Inspirasi: Kerap Normal atau Samar

At total lung capacity (TLC), all lung regions are filled with inhaled air. Attenuation differences between healthy and obstructed lobules are minimal. The scan may appear completely normal or only show subtle centrilobular branch opacities, mild cylindrical bronchiectasis, or mild bronchial wall thickening. Clinicians ordering inspiratory-only CT often falsely rule out pulmonary disease. Pada kapasitas paru total (TLC), seluruh area paru terisi udara inhalasi. Perbedaan densitas antara lobulus sehat dan terobstruksi sangat minimal. Hasil pemindaian dapat tampak normal atau hanya menunjukkan opasitas percabangan sentrilobular samar. Klinisi yang hanya meminta CT inspirasi kerap keliru menyimpulkan tidak ada kelainan paru.

Clinical Pitfall: Up to 50% of biopsy-proven constrictive bronchiolitis cases have normal inspiratory CT scans.
Dynamic Expiration Phase -850 to -950 HU (Trapped)

Expiratory HRCT: Definitive Mosaic Perfusion & Air Trapping HRCT Ekspirasi: Mosaic Perfusion & Air Trapping Definitif

During forced expiration (residual volume), healthy lung parenchyma deflates and increases in radiological attenuation (becomes whiter/denser). Obstructed lobules fail to deflate; air remains trapped, so they stay dark (hypodense, -850 to -950 HU). Furthermore, reflex hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction shunts blood away from hypoventilated lobules, creating stark geographic mosaic perfusion. Selama ekspirasi paksa (volume residu), parenkim paru yang sehat mengempis dan densitas radiologisnya meningkat (tampak lebih putih/padat). Lobulus yang terobstruksi gagal mengempis; udara terperangkap sehingga tetap gelap (hipodens, -850 hingga -950 HU). Vasokonstriksi pulmonal hipoksik refleks mengalihkan darah menjauhi lobulus tersebut, menghasilkan kontras mosaik geografis yang mencolok.

Mandatory Order: Always specify "thin-section (1 mm) paired inspiratory and expiratory HRCT" when evaluating suspected small airway disease.

Histopathology & Surgical Lung Biopsy (VATS) Histopatologi & Biopsi Paru Bedah (VATS)

The gold standard for definitive tissue diagnosis and differentiation from Cryptogenic Organizing Pneumonia (COP/BOOP) Standar emas diagnosis jaringan definitif dan diferensiasi dari Cryptogenic Organizing Pneumonia (COP/BOOP)

Constrictive Bronchiolitis (Popcorn Lung) Bronkiolitis Konstriktif (Popcorn Lung)

  • Anatomic Target: Submucosa of terminal and respiratory membranous bronchioles (<2 mm internal diameter).
  • Morphology: Circumferential, concentric collagenous fibrosis pinching lumen from the outside inward.
  • Lumen Contents: Clean or necrotic debris; absence of intraluminal organizing fibroblast plugs.
  • Steroid Reversibility: Virtually zero; mature cicatrix does not dissolve under corticosteroids.
  • Sampling Pitfall: Patchy distribution requires surgical VATS wedge biopsy; transbronchial biopsy has >70% false negative rate.

Organizing Pneumonia (COP / BOOP) Organizing Pneumonia (COP / BOOP)

  • Anatomic Target: Alveolar ducts and adjacent alveoli with secondary bronchiolar involvement.
  • Morphology: Intraluminal polypoid plugs of young, loose fibroblastic granulation tissue (Masson bodies).
  • Lumen Contents: Packed with myxoid connective tissue plugs within airspaces.
  • Steroid Reversibility: Highly responsive; systemic prednisone typically induces rapid, complete radiologic and clinical resolution.
  • HRCT Appearance: Peripheral/subpleural airspace consolidation, ground-glass opacities, and reverse halo (atoll sign).
