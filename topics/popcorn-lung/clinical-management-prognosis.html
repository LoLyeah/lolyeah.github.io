Clinical Management, Prognostic Trajectory & Differential Diagnostics Tata Laksana Klinis, Trajektori Prognosis & Diagnosis Banding

Because concentric cicatricial fibrosis is structurally irreversible, clinical management centers on aggressive exposure cessation, early macrolide immunomodulation (azithromycin), selective corticosteroid trials, differential exclusion of steroid-responsive mimickers (COP/BOOP), and timely evaluation for bilateral lung transplantation. Karena fibrosis sikatriks konsentris bersifat ireversibel secara struktural, tata laksana klinis berfokus pada penghentian total paparan segera, imunomodulasi makrolida dini (azitromisin), uji kortikosteroid selektif, eksklusi diferensial penyakit yang responsif steroid (COP/BOOP), serta evaluasi tepat waktu untuk transplantasi paru bilateral.

The 4 Evidence-Based Management Pillars 4 Pilar Tata Laksana Berbasis Bukti

Systematic clinical action plan from acute identification to long-term chronic stabilization Rencana aksi klinis sistematis dari identifikasi akut hingga stabilisasi kronis jangka panjang

P1

Absolute Exposure Cessation (Non-Negotiable) Penghentian Mutlak Paparan (Non-Negosiasi)

Immediate, permanent removal from the workplace (popcorn plants, coffee roasting rooms, flavor compounding laboratories) or complete cessation of vaping/e-cigarette aerosols. While obliterated bronchioles cannot regrow, stopping toxic inhalation prevents active dicarbonyl cascade from destroying remaining functional lung units. Continued low-level exposure guarantees progressive lung volume decline. Evakuasi permanen segera dari lingkungan kerja (pabrik popcorn, ruang sangrai kopi, laboratorium perisa) atau penghentian total vaping. Meski bronkiolus yang telah mengalami obliterasi tidak dapat tumbuh kembali, penghentian paparan mencegah kaskade toksik menghancurkan unit paru fungsional yang tersisa.

Key Outcome: Halts active disease progression in 75%+ of occupational cases.
P2

Macrolide Immunomodulation (Azithromycin) Imunomodulasi Makrolida (Azitromisin)

Low-dose long-term azithromycin (250 mg orally, 3 times per week, or 500 mg 3x/week for >6 months) acts via non-antimicrobial immunomodulation. It suppresses IL-8/CXCL8 transcription, reduces neutrophil recruitment and elastase release into the airways, inhibits TGF-β1-driven fibroblast activation, and downregulates mucin hypersecretion. Supported by substantial clinical trials in post-transplant BOS and non-transplant constrictive bronchiolitis. Azitromisin jangka panjang dosis rendah (250 mg per oral, 3 kali seminggu, selama >6 bulan) bekerja melalui imunomodulasi non-antimikroba. Obat ini menekan transkripsi IL-8/CXCL8, mengurangi rekrutmen neutrofil dan pelepasan elastase, menghambat aktivasi fibroblas yang dipicu TGF-β1, serta menurunkan hipersekresi mukus.

Regimen: Azithromycin 250 mg PO on Mon / Wed / Fri · Monitor QTc.
P3

Targeted Corticosteroid Trial & Strict Deprescribing Uji Kortikosteroid Terarah & Depreskripsi Ketat

During early active subacute presentations, an initial trial of oral prednisone (0.5 to 1.0 mg/kg/day, typically 30–60 mg daily for 8–12 weeks) is warranted to treat concurrent lymphocytic bronchiolitis and rule out corticosteroid-responsive organizing pneumonia (COP). Serial spirometry must be obtained at 4, 8, and 12 weeks. If FEV1 fails to improve, steroids must be tapered to prevent steroid toxicity, as mature collagen scars do not respond. Pada fase subakut awal, uji prednison oral (0,5–1,0 mg/kg/hari selama 8–12 minggu) dapat diberikan untuk meredakan bronkiolitis limfositik dan menyingkirkan COP. Spirometri berkala wajib diperiksa pada minggu ke-4, 8, dan 12. Jika FEV1 tidak membaik, steroid harus diturunkan bertahap (tapering) untuk menghindari toksisitas obat.

Rule: If FEV1 change < 10% after 12 weeks, taper off systemic steroids.
P4

Symptom Palliation, ICS/LABA/LAMA & Pulmonary Rehab Paliatif Gejala, ICS/LABA/LAMA & Rehabilitasi Paru

Inhaled corticosteroids combined with long-acting beta-agonists and long-acting muscarinic antagonists (triple therapy: ICS/LABA/LAMA) provide symptomatic airway smooth muscle relaxation for collateral bronchial hyperreactivity. Montelukast (10 mg daily) serves as adjunctive leukotriene blockade. Structured pulmonary rehabilitation and supplemental ambulatory oxygen help manage severe hypoxemia during exertion. Terapi inhalasi kombinasi ICS/LABA/LAMA memberikan relaksasi simptomatik untuk hiperreaktivitas bronkus kolateral. Montelukast (10 mg sehari) bertindak sebagai blokade leukotrien tambahan. Program rehabilitasi paru terstruktur dan suplementasi oksigen ambulatoar penting untuk mengatasi hipoksemia saat beraktivitas.

Supportive: Triple Inhaled Therapy + Montelukast + O2 as indicated.

Non-Occupational Bronchiolitis Obliterans Syndromes (BOS) Sindrom Bronkiolitis Obliterans Non-Okupasional (BOS)

Beyond diacetyl: The clinical spectrum of constrictive bronchiolitis across transplants and pediatric viral infections Di luar diasetil: Spektrum klinis bronkiolitis konstriktif pada transplantasi organ dan infeksi virus anak

CLAD Phenotype

Post-Lung Transplant BOS

The primary manifestation of Chronic Lung Allograft Dysfunction (CLAD). Develops in approximately 50% of bilateral lung transplant recipients within 5 years. Driven by alloimmune T-cell attacks against donor bronchiolar epithelial antigens. Managed via azithromycin, montelukast, and extracorporeal photopheresis (ECP). Manifestasi utama disfungsi allograft paru kronis (CLAD). Muncul pada sekitar 50% resipien transplantasi paru dalam 5 tahun akibat serangan aloimun sel T terhadap antigen epitel bronkiolus donor. Ditata laksana dengan azitromisin, montelukast, dan extracorporeal photopheresis (ECP).

GvHD Manifestation

Post-Stem Cell (HSCT) BOS

Occurs in 2–6% of allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant recipients as a severe pulmonic complication of chronic Graft-versus-Host Disease (cGvHD). Donor T-lymphocytes target host bronchioles. Associated with high mortality; requires systemic immunosuppression optimization (calcineurin inhibitors, ruxolitinib) and azithromycin. Terjadi pada 2–6% resipien transplantasi sel punca hematopoietik (HSCT) alogenik sebagai komplikasi paru berat dari cGvHD. Limfosit T donor menyerang bronkiolus inang. Membutuhkan optimalisasi imunosupresi sistemik (penghambat kalsineurin, ruxolitinib) dan azitromisin.

Pediatric Post-Viral

Post-Infectious Pediatric BO

Most common following severe necrotizing childhood lower respiratory tract infections caused by Adenovirus (serotypes 3, 7, 21), Mycoplasma pneumoniae, or measles. May result in unilateral Swyer-James-MacLeod syndrome, characterized by a small, hyperlucent lung on radiography with hypoplastic pulmonary vascularity. Paling sering terjadi pasca-infeksi saluran napas bawah nekrotikan berat pada anak yang disebabkan Adenovirus (serotipe 3, 7, 21), Mycoplasma pneumoniae, atau campak. Dapat berkembang menjadi sindrom Swyer-James-MacLeod dengan gambaran paru hiperlusen unilateral.
