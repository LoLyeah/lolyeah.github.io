Key Trends & Market Insights from GIIAS 2026 Tren Utama & Wawasan Pasar GIIAS 2026

1. The Sub-300M EV Disruption & Rebranding 1. Disrupsi & Penamaan EV Sub-300 Juta

Chinese EV manufacturers (Wuling, BYD, Changan, Geely, Jaecoo, Aion) have established a dominant presence under IDR 300 Million. BYD adapted its global Seagull micro-EV under the BYD Atto 1 nameplate for the Indonesian market, launching entry trims around Rp 195 - 200 Million with Blade Battery tech. Pabrikan EV asal Tiongkok (Wuling, BYD, Changan, Geely, Jaecoo, Aion) menguasai segmen di bawah Rp 300 Juta. BYD secara khusus memasarkan micro-EV global Seagull dengan nama resmi BYD Atto 1 untuk pasar Indonesia di kisaran Rp 195 - 200 Juta berbekal teknologi baterai Blade.

2. Suspension Engineering Trade-offs 2. Kompromi Rekayasa Suspensi

While budget entry models (Brio S, S-Presso, Ayla) rely on simple MacPherson & Torsion Beam setups to minimize costs, EVs in the 240M-450M range (Geely EX2 Pro, Jaecoo J5, Leapmotor C10) utilize independent multi-link rear suspensions to compensate for heavy battery curb weights and deliver refined ride quality. Mobil budget entry (Brio S, S-Presso, Ayla) menggunakan MacPherson & Torsion Beam untuk menekan biaya. Sebaliknya, EV di rentang 240M-450M (Geely EX2 Pro, Jaecoo J5, Leapmotor C10) menyematkan suspensi independen multi-link untuk menangani bobot baterai dan menjaga kenyamanan berkendara.

3. Luxury Booth Entry Positioning 3. Posisi Entry Booth Mobil Mewah

For premium brands like Lexus (LBX @ Rp 914M), BMW (218i @ <1B), Mercedes-Benz (GLB 200 @ Rp 1,49B), and Volvo (XC60 @ Rp 1,5B), the booth entry model serves as a gateway product to onboard younger buyers into their ecosystem without diluting brand prestige. Bagi merek premium seperti Lexus (LBX @ Rp 914M), BMW (218i @ <1B), Mercedes-Benz (GLB 200 @ Rp 1,49B), dan Volvo (XC60 @ Rp 1,5B), mobil termurah berfungsi sebagai pintu masuk untuk menarik konsumen muda tanpa mengorbankan prestise merek.
