Key Trends & Market Insights from GIIAS 2026 Tren Utama & Wawasan Pasar GIIAS 2026

1. The Sub-300M EV Disruption 1. Disrupsi EV Sub-300 Juta

Chinese EV manufacturers (Wuling, BYD, Changan, Geely, Jaecoo, Aion) have established a dominant presence under IDR 300 Million. Features previously reserved for luxury cars—such as multi-link rear suspension, Blade batteries, and 360-degree cameras—are now accessible in entry-level EV offerings. Pabrikan EV asal Tiongkok (Wuling, BYD, Changan, Geely, Jaecoo, Aion) menguasai segmen di bawah Rp 300 Juta. Fitur yang sebelumnya hanya ada di mobil mewah—seperti suspensi belakang multi-link, baterai Blade, dan kamera 360—kini hadir pada EV entry-level.

2. Suspension Engineering Trade-offs 2. Kompromi Rekayasa Suspensi

While budget entry models (Brio, S-Presso, Ayla) rely on simple MacPherson & Torsion Beam setups to minimize costs, EVs in the 240M-450M range (Geely EX2 Pro, Jaecoo J5, Leapmotor C10) utilize independent multi-link rear suspensions to compensate for heavy battery curb weights and deliver refined ride quality. Mobil budget entry (Brio, S-Presso, Ayla) menggunakan MacPherson & Torsion Beam untuk menekan biaya. Sebaliknya, EV di rentang 240M-450M (Geely EX2 Pro, Jaecoo J5, Leapmotor C10) menyematkan suspensi independen multi-link untuk menangani bobot baterai dan menjaga kenyamanan berkendara.

3. Luxury Booth Entry Pricing 3. Posisi Entry Booth Mobil Mewah

For premium brands like Lexus (LBX @ 914M), BMW (218i @ <1B), Mercedes-Benz (GLB 200 @ 1.49B), and Volvo (XC60 @ 1.5B), the booth entry model serves as a gateway product to onboard younger buyers into their ecosystem without diluting brand prestige. Bagi merek premium seperti Lexus (LBX @ 914M), BMW (218i @ <1B), Mercedes-Benz (GLB 200 @ 1.49B), dan Volvo (XC60 @ 1.5B), mobil termurah berfungsi sebagai pintu masuk untuk menarik konsumen muda tanpa mengorbankan prestise merek.
