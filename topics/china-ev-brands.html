Why China Dominates the Global EV Paradigm

Mengapa Tiongkok Mendominasi Paradigma EV Global

China's electrification lead is not merely a product of lower labor costs; it is the result of unprecedented supply chain verticality. From mining and refining over 70% of the world's lithium, cobalt, and graphite, to Tier-1 battery giants like CATL and FinDreams, Chinese automakers achieve rapid iterative cycles—updating models every 12 to 18 months compared to 5 to 7 years in traditional legacy Western OEM schedules.

Keunggulan elektrifikasi Tiongkok bukan semata-mata karena efisiensi biaya produksi, melainkan integrasi vertikal rantai pasok yang tak tertandingi. Menguasai lebih dari 70% pemrosesan mineral baterai global, didukung raksasa sel seperti CATL dan FinDreams, produsen mobil Tiongkok mampu memperbarui siklus produk setiap 12–18 bulan dibanding 5–7 tahun pada pabrikan konvensional.

The Strategic Rise of EREV & Dual Powertrains

Lonjakan Strategis EREV & Fleksibilitas Powertrain

While pure BEVs lead urban commuting, Extended-Range Electric Vehicles (EREV) and dedicated hybrid transmissions (BYD DM-i 5.0, Geely NordThor, Chery C-DM) have emerged as the fastest-growing market catalyst. By decoupling the gasoline engine from mechanical drivewheels and using it solely as an onboard electric generator, drivers achieve 1,200 km to 2,100 km of continuous driving without charging bottleneck anxieties.

Meskipun BEV mendominasi mobilitas harian, kendaraan Extended-Range (EREV) dan sistem super hybrid (BYD DM-i 5.0, Geely NordThor, Chery C-DM) menjadi motor pertumbuhan paling pesat. Dengan mesin bensin murni sebagai generator pengisi daya baterai, pengguna menikmati jarak tempuh total 1.200 hingga 2.100 km tanpa rasa cemas antrean SPKLU.
