Comparing EV Battery Cell Architectures

Every chemistry presents distinct trade-offs between volumetric density, raw material vulnerability, thermal runaway thresholds, and manufacturing cost.

LFP / LMFP

Lithium Iron Phosphate

  • Cost-effective, zero cobalt/nickel dependency
  • Exceptional thermal stability (>270°C runaway temp)
  • High cycle life (3,000 – 6,000+ cycles)
  • CTP (Cell-to-Pack) integration compensates lower gravimetric density (~160-205 Wh/kg)
NMC 811 / NCMA

Nickel Manganese Cobalt

  • High gravimetric energy density (250 – 300+ Wh/kg)
  • Superior cold-weather discharge performance
  • Premium long-range EVs (Hyundai Ioniq, Porsche Taycan)
  • Higher raw material cost; vulnerable to nickel/cobalt market swings
Sodium-Ion (Na-Ion)

Sodium-Ion Technology

  • Zero lithium required; abundant, low-cost raw materials
  • Exceptional sub-zero operation retention (90% at -20°C)
  • Gravimetric density ~140-170 Wh/kg
  • Ideal for entry city cars, 2-wheelers & energy storage
Solid-State (SSB)

Solid Electrolyte Batteries

  • Replaces liquid electrolyte with solid polymer/ceramic
  • Ultra-high energy density (400 – 500 Wh/kg)
  • Eliminates thermal runaway fire risks completely
  • Pilot production phase in 2026; high manufacturing cost
