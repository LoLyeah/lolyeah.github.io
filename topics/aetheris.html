Mathematical Foundations Fondasi Matematis

Core equations governing wave kinematics, dispersion, foam generation, and underwater light absorption. Persamaan inti yang mengatur kinematika gelombang, dispersi, pembentukan busa, dan serapan cahaya bawah air.

Dispersion Kinematics

Deep-Water Dispersion Relation

ω²(k) = g • k • tanh(k • d) ≈ g • k

In deep water where depth \(d\) exceeds half the wavelength (\(d > \lambda/2\)), phase velocity \(c = \omega / k = \sqrt{g / k}\) increases with wavelength, causing long storm swells to outrun short choppy wind waves. Pada perairan dalam di mana kedalaman \(d\) melebihi setengah panjang gelombang (\(d > \lambda/2\)), kecepatan fase \(c = \omega / k = \sqrt{g / k}\) bertambah seiring panjang gelombang, menyebabkan alun badai panjang mendahului ombak angin pendek.

Jacobian Eigenvalues

Wave Crest Folding & Foam

det(J) = (1 - λ∂D_x/∂x)(1 - λ∂D_z/∂z) - λ²(∂D_x/∂z)(∂D_z/∂x) < 0

When horizontal choppiness displacement causes the surface transformation Jacobian determinant to become negative, wave crests self-intersect and break. Aetheris (v2) detects this threshold to spawn persistent whitecap foam. Ketika pergeseran horizontal menyebabkan determinan Jacobian transformasi permukaan bernilai negatif, puncak gelombang saling berpotongan dan pecah. Aetheris (v2) mendeteksi ambang batas ini untuk menghasilkan busa putih persisten.

Radiative Transfer

Beer-Lambert Water Extinction

I(λ, d) = I_0(λ) • exp(-σ_ext(λ) • d)

Red light (\(\lambda pprox 700 ext{ nm}\)) attenuates rapidly within 5 meters, leaving deep sapphire-cyan tones. Subsurface scattering is approximated by forward-scattering phase functions near wave crests. Cahaya merah (\(\lambda pprox 700 ext{ nm}\)) melemah dengan cepat dalam kedalaman 5 meter, menyisakan warna safir-sian yang dalam. Hamburan bawah air diaproksimasi melalui fungsi fase hamburan maju di sekitar puncak gelombang.
