Botanical Relicts of Socotra: Endemic Taxa & Frankincense Flora Relik Socotra: Spesies Endemik & Kemenyan

Socotra harbors one of the highest densities of island plant endemism in the Old World. Isolated since the Oligocene, its vegetation survived mass continental extinctions, preserving bizarre pachycaul morphologies, a global epicenter of 11 frankincense species, and succulent adaptations tuned to monsoonal fog cycles. Socotra memiliki salah satu kerapatan tumbuhan endemik pulau tertinggi di Dunia Lama. Terisolasi sejak zaman Oligosen, floranya selamat dari kepunahan massal benua, melestarikan morfologi batang gembung (pachycaul), pusat 11 spesies kemenyan dunia, dan adaptasi sukulen terhadap kabut muson.

The 11 Endemic Boswellia Species 11 Spesies Kemenyan (Boswellia) Endemik

Out of roughly 25 frankincense species known to science worldwide, an astounding 11 are native exclusively to Socotra Island, exhibiting extreme micro-habitat speciation. Dari sekitar 25 spesies pohon kemenyan di seluruh dunia, 11 spesies di antaranya hanya hidup di Pulau Socotra, menunjukkan spesiasi habitat mikro yang luar biasa.

Apocynaceae • Bottle Tree

Adenium socotranum

The caudex forms a swollen conical reservoir that stores water under high osmotic pressure. The waxy, reflective silver-grey bark reflects intense desert solar radiation, preventing core overheating. Batang membentuk reservoir kerucut membesar yang menyimpan air di bawah tekanan osmotik tinggi. Kulit kayu abu-abu keperakan memantulkan radiasi surya terik, mencegah pemanasan jaringan internal.

Cucurbitaceae • Cucumber Tree

Dendrosicyos socotranus

A phylogenetic anomaly. Instead of remaining a soft climbing vine like all other cucumbers, it evolved a giant fibrous trunk of parenchymatous water-storage cells capable of standing 6 meters tall. Anomali filogenetik. Alih-alih merambat layaknya keluarga mentimun biasa, tanaman ini berevolusi membentuk batang kayu berserat sel parenkim raksasa setinggi 6 meter.

Moraceae • Fig Family Succulent

Dorstenia gigas

Related to edible figs and mulberries, this bizarre pachycaul grows exclusively on vertical karstic limestone sea cliffs, securing its bulbous base into hairline cracks inaccessible to grazing animals. Kerabat dekat buah ara (tin) dan murbei, tanaman pachycaul aneh ini hidup khusus di tebing batu kapur vertikal tepi laut, menancapkan batangnya di celah yang tak terjangkau hewan pemakan rumput.

Conservation Urgency • The Geriatric Forest

Botanists walking through Socotra’s ancient Dracaena groves notice a haunting paradox: almost every single Dragon's Blood Tree is between 200 and 500 years old. There are virtually no young saplings surviving in the open wilderness. The species is facing demographic collapse due to three intertwined factors: Para botanis yang menjelajahi hutan pohon naga Socotra menemukan paradoks mengerikan: hampir seluruh pohon Dracaena berusia antara 200 hingga 500 tahun. Nyaris tidak ada anakan pohon muda yang selamat di alam liar. Spesies ini menghadapi kepunahan demografis akibat tiga faktor utama:

1. Feral Goat Overgrazing 1. Overgrazing Kambing Liar

Introduced domestic goats devour every emerging tender seedling before it can develop fibrous, unpalatable trunk bark. Populasi kambing liar memakan habis setiap tunas bibit muda sebelum sempat membentuk kulit kayu tebal yang keras.

2. Catastrophic Cyclones 2. Badai Siklon Dahsyat

In 2015, Tropical Cyclones Chapala and Megh made unprecedented direct landfall within one week, violently snapping thousands of centuries-old trees. Tahun 2015, Badai Tropis Chapala dan Megh menerjang berturut-turut dalam seminggu, merobohkan ribuan pohon purba berusia berabad-abad.

3. Cloud Ceiling Rise 3. Kenaikan Batas Awan Kabut

Regional ocean warming has raised the condensation cloud base, depriving lower plateau plateaus of essential morning fog mists. Pemanasan suhu laut menaikkan ketinggian kondensasi awan, mengurangi pasokan kabut basah pagi bagi pohon di dataran rendah.
