Biological Inspiration vs. Mathematical Reality Inspirasi Biologis vs. Realitas Matematis

The Calculus of Forward & Backward Propagation Kalkulus Propagasi Maju & Mundur

A multi-layer neural network is fundamentally a parameterized composition of non-linear vector functions. Without non-linear activations, depth collapses into a single trivial linear regression. Jaringan saraf multilapis pada hakikatnya adalah komposisi berparameter dari fungsi-fungsi vektor non-linear. Tanpa aktivasi non-linear, berapapun kedalaman lapisannya akan runtuh menjadi regresi linear sederhana.

Universal Approximation Theorem

Formalized by George Cybenko (1989) for sigmoid activations and Kurt Hornik (1991) for arbitrary bounded continuous non-linearities, the theorem proves that a feedforward network with a single hidden layer containing finite neurons can approximate any continuous function on compact subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$ to arbitrary precision $\epsilon > 0$.

However, the theorem provides an existence proof, not an efficiency proof. Achieving target precision with a single flat hidden layer requires an exponentially exploding number of neurons ($O(2^n)$), whereas deep hierarchical architectures express compositionality exponentially more efficiently ($O(n)$ parameters per layer).

F(x) = \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_i \sigma\left(w_i^T x + b_i\right) \quad \text{such that} \quad |F(x) - f(x)| < \epsilon

The Chain Rule Across Layers

Training requires minimizing a scalar empirical risk loss function $\mathcal{L}(\theta)$ over parameters $\theta = \{W, b\}$. Backpropagation computes the partial derivatives $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W^{[l]}}$ by recursive application of the multivariable chain rule from output layer $L$ backwards to input layer $1$.

Defining error sensitivity vector $\delta^{[l]} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z^{[l]}}$, the recurrence relation propagates backwards as an element-wise Hadamard product $\odot$ with the local activation derivative:

\delta^{[l]} = \left((W^{[l+1]})^T \delta^{[l+1]}\right) \odot \sigma'\left(z^{[l]}\right) \\ \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W^{[l]}} = \delta^{[l]} (a^{[l-1]})^T, \quad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b^{[l]}} = \delta^{[l]}

Taxonomy of 12 Paradigm Classes Taksonomi 12 Kelas Paradigma Arsitektur

Every neural architecture is defined by its inductive biases—assumptions embedded into the tensor graph structure that enable sample-efficient generalization on specific data modalities. Setiap arsitektur jaringan saraf ditentukan oleh bias induktifnya—asumsi yang tertanam dalam struktur graf tensor yang memungkinkan generalisasi efisien pada modalitas data tertentu.

Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA)

Introduced in DeepSeek-V2 and scaled in DeepSeek-V3, Multi-Head Latent Attention compresses Key and Value projections into a shared low-rank latent vector $c_t^{KV} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_c}$ where $d_c \ll n_{heads} \times d_k$.

During autoregressive inference, only the compressed latent vector is cached in VRAM rather than high-dimensional $K$ and $V$ matrices. This yields a staggering 93.3% reduction in Key-Value cache memory footprint without sacrificing attention expressivity.

Test-Time Compute & Reasoning RL

Pioneered by OpenAI o1/o3 and DeepSeek-R1 (2024–2026), the frontier paradigm shifts from pre-training compute scaling to inference-time reasoning allocation. Through pure reinforcement learning without supervised human fine-tuning, models discover Chain-of-Thought (CoT) self-correction and backtracking.

Rich Sutton's "Bitter Lesson" now applies to inference: models that spend minutes exploring multiple solution trajectories, verifying proofs with code sandboxes, and pruning erroneous branches dramatically outperform single-pass autoregressive decoders on competition mathematics and systems programming.
