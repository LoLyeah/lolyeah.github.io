Land vs. Water Logistics: The Physics, Economics & Operational Truth

Logistik Darat vs. Air: Fisika, Ekonomi & Realitas Operasional

Is land-based logistics inefficient compared to water-based freight? In raw energy thermodynamics, moving cargo by water is up to 50 times more efficient than road trucking and 4 times more efficient than rail. Yet in real-world supply chains, water logistics frequently loses. This compendium resolves the physical law against the economic friction.

Benarkah logistik darat jauh lebih tidak efisien dibandingkan logistik berbasis air? Dari sisi termodinamika energi murni, memindahkan kargo via air 10 hingga 50 kali lebih hemat energi dibanding truk dan 4 kali lebih hemat dibanding kereta api. Namun di rantai pasok nyata, logistik air sering kali kalah. Kompendium ini membedah hukum fisika melawan friksi ekonomi.

Why Archimedes Beats Friction: The Physics of Buoyant Freight

Mengapa Archimedes Mengalahkan Gesekan: Fisika Angkutan Terapung

The staggering energy disparity between water and land logistics boils down to basic physical constants: gravitational load support and contact friction. On land, every kilogram of freight must be supported against gravity by physical structural materials. In a tractor-trailer, 40 tonnes rest on 18 pneumatic rubber tires. As those tires rotate at 90 km/h under 8 bar of pressure, the rubber continuously deforms and relaxes, causing severe hysteretic heat loss with a rolling friction coefficient (Crr) of 0.007 to 0.010.

Kesenjangan energi dramatis antara logistik air dan darat berakar pada konstanta fisika fundamental: penopang beban gravitasi dan gesekan kontak. Di darat, setiap kilogram muatan harus ditopang melawan gravitasi oleh material struktural fisik. Pada truk tronton atau semi-trailer, 40 ton bobot bertumpu pada 18 ban karet pneumatik. Saat ban berputar pada kecepatan 90 km/jam di bawah tekanan 8 bar, karet terus mengalami deformasi dan relaksasi siklik yang memicu kehilangan panas histeresis dengan koefisien gesek (Crr) 0,007 hingga 0,010.

Freight rail dramatically improves on this: hard steel wheels on hardened steel rails reduce rolling resistance to Crr ≈ 0.0015, an 80% friction reduction over trucks. Yet rail must still fight track bed deflection and mechanical axle journal resistance.

Kereta api barang secara signifikan memperbaiki ini: roda baja keras di atas rel baja keras menekan tahanan gelinding menjadi Crr ≈ 0,0015, penurunan friksi sebesar 80% dibanding truk. Namun kereta api tetap harus menanggung defleksi bantalan rel dan friksi bantalan gandar mekanis.

By contrast, a water vessel pays zero mechanical energy to support its vertical mass. By Archimedes' principle (Fb = ρ · g · ∇), the displaced seawater counterbalances the entire displacement for free. The ship faces only hydrodynamic resistance: viscous boundary layer skin friction governed by the ITTC-1957 correlation, and wave-making drag. Because wave drag scales with the fourth power of the Froude number (Fn = v / √(g·L)), modern 400-meter container ships operating at slow-steaming speeds of 14–17 knots maintain a Froude number below 0.15, where wave-making resistance is almost negligible.

Sebaliknya, kapal air membayar nol energi mekanis untuk menopang massa vertikalnya. Berdasarkan hukum Archimedes (Fb = ρ · g · ∇), air laut yang dipindahkan menyeimbangkan seluruh bobot kapal secara gratis. Hambatan kapal murni berupa resistansi hidrodinamika: gesekan batas viskos fluida (skin friction ITTC-1957) dan gelombang haluan. Karena hambatan gelombang berskala eksponensial terhadap angka Froude (Fn = v / √(g·L)), kapal kontainer 400 meter berlayar hemat (slow steaming 14–17 knot) memiliki angka Froude di bawah 0,15, di mana pembentukan gelombang hampir tidak memakan energi.

The Economic Paradox: Why Trucks Win at Short Distances

Paradoks Ekonomi: Mengapa Truk Menang pada Jarak Dekat

If water transport is up to 50 times more energy-efficient and 15 times cheaper per line-haul ton-kilometer, why are highways clogged with freight trucks while coastal barges sit underutilized? This is the central economic paradox of transport geography, governed by fixed intermodal transfer costs and inventory carrying speed.

Jika angkutan air hingga 50 kali lebih hemat energi dan 15 kali lebih murah per ton-km pelayaran, mengapa jalan tol tetap dipadati truk barang sementara tongkang pesisir kurang dimanfaatkan? Inilah paradoks ekonomi utama geografi transportasi, yang dikendalikan oleh biaya tetap alih moda (intermodal penalty) dan kecepatan perputaran modal barang.

A road truck provides seamless point-to-point service: it backs up to the factory loading dock in Karawang, loads 20 tonnes of goods, and drives directly to a distribution center in Cikarang or Bandung. There is zero modal transshipment.

Truk jalan raya menyediakan layanan mulus dari pintu ke pintu: mundur langsung ke dok muat pabrik di Karawang, memuat 20 ton barang, dan melaju langsung ke pusat distribusi di Cikarang atau Bandung. Tidak ada alih muat antarmoda sama sekali.

To move those same goods by coastal ship or barge requires:

Untuk memindahkan barang yang sama menggunakan kapal pesisir atau tongkang, prosesnya memerlukan:

  • First-mile drayage trucking from factory to seaport ($100–$250)
  • Angkutan drayage truk first-mile dari pabrik ke pelabuhan ($100–$250)
  • Terminal Handling Charge (THC) and container crane gantry lifts ($120–$220)
  • Biaya bongkar muat kontainer (THC) dan gantry crane ($120–$220)
  • Vessel loading, stowage planning, customs clearance, and port dwell waiting time (24–72 hours)
  • Pemuatan kapal, stowage plan, bea cukai, dan waktu tunggu sandar pelabuhan (24–72 jam)
  • Discharge crane lift at destination port plus last-mile drayage truck to warehouse ($200–$350)
  • Bongkar crane di pelabuhan tujuan plus truk last-mile ke gudang tujuan ($200–$350)

This creates a large fixed cost hurdle (Cterminal ≈ $450 – $800 per container). Below the break-even distance of approximately 300 to 500 kilometers, the variable fuel savings of ocean freight are completely overwhelmed by the fixed intermodal transfer penalty.

Ini menciptakan dinding biaya tetap yang tinggi (Cterminal ≈ $450 – $800 per kontainer). Di bawah jarak impas sekitar 300 hingga 500 kilometer, penghematan bahan bakar pelayaran sepenuhnya ditelan oleh penalti alih moda tersebut.
